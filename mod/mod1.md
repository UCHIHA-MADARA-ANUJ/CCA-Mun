**Speaking time: 60s · 172 words ≈ 60-62s at 165-175 wpm · emergency cut: drop "The soil still carries it." (−5 words) if the chair is strict**

---

## THE SPEECH (v2 — 173 words)

> A mother on Runit Island checks the tide before her children swim. The tide carries the lagoon. The lagoon carries the tomb.
>
> **THE TOMB IS CRACKING.** One hundred twenty thousand tons of radioactive waste — buried by one country, abandoned by the same. A tribunal awarded **two point three billion dollars** — and ran out of money. **Half the claimants died waiting.**
>
> **Kazakhstan.** Four hundred fifty-six tests. **Two hundred fifty times the yield of Hiroshima.** The soil still carries it. People still live downwind.
>
> The island sits two meters above the sea. The sea is rising.
>
> **In 1954, fallout reached a Japanese boat one hundred thirty kilometres from Bikini. Never told. One of them died.**
>
> **THE FISH DO NOT ASK WHERE THE CESIUM CAME FROM. THE RAIN DOES NOT ASK PERMISSION TO FALL.**
>
> **The ground is the evidence.** WILL THIS COMMITTEE BUILD THE MACHINERY — **or watch the tomb fall?**
>
> **JAPAN'S ANSWER IS NOT ANOTHER SPEECH. IT IS MACHINERY.** Measure. Warn. Clean. **Or be recorded explaining why you would not.**
>
> **THE TIDE DOES NOT WAIT.**
>
> **I yield.**

## IN-CAUCUS KNIVES (after the opener lands)

**If France/UK/Russia/DPRK try to water it down:**
> "The delegate speaks of 'balanced assessment.' The balance in the record is one-sided: two thousand tests, and the ground still holds the poison. We are not balancing — we are measuring what was left."

**If a testing state says "the past is the past":**
> "The past ended eighty years ago. The contamination did not. Runit Dome is cracking in the present tense."

**If China/US try to redirect to civil energy (the ALPS ambush):**
> "The distinguished delegate may raise civilian energy as its own motion. This caucus is about the weapons enterprise — the agenda said 'proliferation,' and we are discussing proliferation."

**If someone asks "who pays?":**
> "Japan's text names no state and forces nothing. It invites contribution through existing mechanisms — the IAEA network, the CTBTO system, voluntary funds. The question is not who is forced; it is who chooses to be recorded."

**If Kazakhstan speaks (bloc member — her testimony is our hammer):**
> "Kazakhstan has carried this burden for four decades. Japan asks this committee to stop watching her carry it alone."

**If the US counters on the Marshalls — "the Compact compensated them":**
> "A Compact signed after the damage is a ledger, not a permission slip. The dome was built without the consent of the people who live beneath its shadow. The record shows the tests were never asked for."

**If the US attacks first (Runit is then fair game — SAFE version, no $4M claim):**
> "The distinguished delegate of the United States speaks of responsibility. A tribunal awarded two point three billion dollars — and ran out of money before half the claimants were paid. That is not a number. That is the record."

> ⚠️ **Do NOT say "America paid four million."** It is contested (the US counters with COFA payments of ~$600M+). The tribunal-running-out-of-money framing does the same damage and cannot be countered.
