# MOD 3 — SOLUTION MACHINERY FOR TRANSBOUNDARY RADIOLOGICAL THREATS

## Delegation of Japan · CCA MUN ’26 · UNGA

**Recommended solution topic:** Developing a consent-based framework for coordinated monitoring, cross-border notification, and measurable remediation of weapons-related radiological threats

**Speaking time:** 60 seconds

**Speech length:** 147 words — calibrated for an aggressive 60-second delivery

**Status:** Final, fact-audited version · 21 August 2026

---

## SPEECH

[HARD]

Enough diagnosis. The instruments exist—and remain disconnected.

[FAST — evidence]

CTBTO’s planned 337-facility network—roughly ninety percent operating—detected all six declared DPRK tests. IAEA can carry warnings. RANET can mobilize requested experts and equipment. ENVIRONET can guide remediation.

[FULL FORCE]

**AND YET: DETECTION STOPS AT A DATABASE. WARNING WAITS FOR POLITICS. FUNDING IS COUNTED BEFORE CLEANUP IS MEASURED.**

[THREE STRIKES]

**MONITOR:** one standard for radionuclides, units, detection limits, uncertainty and chain of custody.

**RESPOND:** credible evidence of a plausible cross-border pathway triggers warning. **Warn first. Attribute later.**

**REMEDIATE:** request-based assistance, community participation and public outcome indicators—not ceremonial cheques.

[CLIPPED]

No compulsory inspection. No weapon designs. No automatic liability.

[PROOF]

Japan already hosts an IAEA RANET capacity-building centre. Not theory—existing infrastructure.

[FINAL FORCE]

**A SYSTEM THAT DETECTS CONTAMINATION BUT CANNOT CARRY IT THROUGH WARNING TO CLEANUP IS NOT PROTECTION.**

**IT IS DOCUMENTATION OF FAILURE.**

**CONNECT THE MACHINERY. MEASURE THE CLEANUP. END THE EXCUSE.**

[DROP]

I yield.

## POINT-OF-ORDER FIREWALL

**If challenged that all 337 CTBTO facilities are not operating:**

> “Japan said ‘planned 337-facility network’ and ‘roughly ninety percent operating’. That is the CTBTO’s official description.”

**If challenged that CTBTO detects explosions, not contamination:**

> “Correct. The speech attributes test detection to CTBTO. Environmental monitoring and contamination assessment enter through national systems, IRMIS, laboratories and Japan’s proposed interoperable chain.”

**If challenged that CTBTO data are not universally public:**

> “Japan made no public-access claim. Data use remains subject to CTBTO arrangements and authorized channels.”

**If challenged that RANET deploys automatically:**

> “Japan explicitly called the assistance request-based. RANET mobilizes registered capabilities when assistance is requested and accepted.”

**If challenged that ENVIRONET is a cleanup fund:**

> “Japan said ENVIRONET can guide remediation. It is an expertise and cooperation network, not a compulsory fund.”

**If accused of duplicating existing systems:**

> “Japan assigns each institution only its existing function: CTBTO detects, IEC communicates, RANET assists, ENVIRONET supports remediation knowledge. The proposal connects outputs; it does not duplicate institutions.”

**If challenged on the notification threshold:**

> “The threshold is a Japanese policy proposal informed by the design logic of international emergency standards. It is not falsely presented as an existing universal weapons-law obligation.”

**If accused of violating sovereignty:**

> “Site access is consent-based and assistance is request-based. Notification concerns plausible effects on another State; it does not confer inspection authority.”

**If accused of assigning liability:**

> “The speech expressly states ‘no automatic liability’. Warning and assistance occur without prejudging attribution, compensation or legal responsibility.”

**If attacked on Fukushima:**

> “This MOD concerns weapons-related radiological threats under the committee agenda. Japan nevertheless uses international monitoring and RANET capacity-building in civilian safety contexts, proving that its evidence standard is consistent.”

## FINAL SAFETY RULE

Japan is not proposing a new supranational nuclear authority. It is proposing interoperability:

> CTBTO detects. IAEA channels warnings. RANET assists. ENVIRONET supports remediation. States retain consent. Public indicators prove whether the solution worked.
