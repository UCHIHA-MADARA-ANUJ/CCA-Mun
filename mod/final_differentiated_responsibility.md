# FINAL MOD — DIFFERENTIATED RESPONSIBILITY FOR RADIOLOGICAL HARM

## Delegation of Japan · CCA MUN ’26 · UNGA

**Topic:** Determining differentiated responsibilities of nuclear-armed States, extended-deterrence beneficiaries, and non-nuclear disarmament advocates for monitoring, victim assistance, and remediation of transboundary radiological harm, without prejudging legal liability

**Speaking time:** 75 seconds

**Speech length:** 188 words — calibrated for a fast, controlled 75-second delivery averaging approximately 150 words per minute

**Status:** Final named-target, maximum-aggression, fact-audited version · 22 August 2026

---

## SPEECH

[HARD — no greeting]

Some States own the weapon. Some shelter beneath it. Others build moral authority by opposing it. Every category wants authority. Every category gets responsibility.

[SELF-INOCULATE — controlled]

Japan will not hide: we benefit from US extended deterrence and accept responsibility. Japan hosts an IAEA RANET Capacity-Building Centre for emergency-response training.

[THREE STRIKES]

Our test: **SOURCE. BENEFIT. CAPACITY.**

Source States preserve and release non-sensitive environmental records and lead remediation. Beneficiaries provide warning nodes, laboratories and emergency teams. Capable advocates provide victim assistance, experts and independent review.

[NAMED ATTACK — build through all three]

Norway: NATO says Allies are protected by nuclear deterrence, while nuclear sharing distributes benefits, responsibilities and risks. **YOU CANNOT POCKET NUCLEAR PROTECTION AND OUTSOURCE ECOLOGICAL RESPONSIBILITY.**

Austria: hosting institutions is not implementation. **A VIENNA ADDRESS IS NOT A VICTIM-ASSISTANCE PROGRAMME.**

Israel: strategic ambiguity may protect declaratory policy. **IT CANNOT JUSTIFY AMBIGUITY IN EMERGENCY WARNING.**

[LEGAL FIREWALL — clipped]

No weapon designs. No equal burdens. No prejudgment of liability.

[MECHANISM]

Register contributions; credit existing work; measure victims assisted and land restored.

[VERDICT — climb]

Source responsibility remains primary. But no automatic liability does not mean no responsibility to act.

[FINAL FORCE]

**IF YOU OWN THE RISK, BENEFIT FROM IT, OR CAN REDUCE IT—STOP EXPLAINING. START CONTRIBUTING.**

[DROP]

I yield.

## WHY THE THREE NAMED STRIKES ARE SAFE

### Norway

- Norway is a NATO ally protected by NATO’s nuclear-deterrence posture.
- NATO has stated that the benefits, responsibilities and risks of nuclear deterrence are shared across the Alliance.
- The speech does **not** claim that Norway hosts nuclear weapons or bears legal liability for another State’s testing.
- Japan converts NATO’s burden-sharing language into a proposed environmental contribution, then asks Norway to match benefit with action.

### Austria

- Austria hosts major international nuclear institutions in Vienna and is a prominent humanitarian-disarmament advocate.
- The speech does **not** claim that Austria contributes nothing or controls the IAEA or CTBTO.
- It distinguishes institutional location from a measurable Austrian national contribution to victim assistance and remediation.

### Israel

- Israel maintains an official policy of nuclear ambiguity and neither confirms nor denies possession.
- The speech does **not** call an external arsenal estimate official or require an admission of possession.
- It asks for emergency-warning participation that can preserve counts, locations, designs and doctrine.

## POINT-OF-ORDER FIREWALL

**If Norway says NATO’s wording creates no environmental duty:**

> “Correct: Japan did not present it as existing treaty law. Japan proposes that a formally shared security benefit carry a supplementary implementation contribution, while source responsibility remains primary.”

**If Norway says it already supports verification and RANET:**

> “Japan credits every existing contribution. Norway should place that capability in the responsibility register, identify what is deployable and accept measurable outcomes.”

**If Austria says it already contributes to international institutions:**

> “Those contributions receive full credit. Japan’s question remains specific: which Austrian national funding, expertise or deployable capability will reach affected communities?”

**If Austria says source States must pay instead:**

> “Agreed that source responsibility remains primary. Capacity responsibility is proportional assistance, not equal liability or a subsidy that erases the source burden.”

**If Israel says the speech labels it a nuclear-armed State:**

> “Japan made no possession claim. It referred only to Israel’s acknowledged declaratory ambiguity and proposed an emergency route requiring no arsenal admission, count, location or doctrine.”

**If Israel invokes national security:**

> “The speech expressly protects weapon designs and requires no arsenal declaration. An emergency-warning channel can protect secrecy without concealing a cross-border hazard.”

**If Japan is accused of hypocrisy under the US umbrella:**

> “Japan expressly acknowledged its extended-deterrence benefit and accepted Benefit and Capacity responsibility. Its RANET centre, training and international assistance show implementation rather than exemption.”

**If a source State says Japan is equalizing all categories:**

> “The speech says the opposite: no equal burdens, and source responsibility remains primary. Beneficiary and capacity contributions supplement; they do not dilute the source burden.”

**If challenged that assistance implies liability:**

> “No. Japan expressly rejects prejudgment of liability. Monitoring, victim assistance and remediation can proceed while attribution, compensation and legal claims remain separately determined.”

## FINAL STRATEGIC RULE

The speech does not accuse three States at random. Each named delegation represents one escape route:

- **Norway:** security benefit without environmental implementation;
- **Austria:** moral leadership without a measurable national contribution;
- **Israel:** strategic ambiguity extended into emergency cooperation.

Japan destroys all three escapes only after admitting its own category and accepting a contribution. That self-inoculation is what makes the attack credible.
