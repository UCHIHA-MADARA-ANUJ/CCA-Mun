# MOD 2 — THE LOOPHOLES ARE DECISIONS

## Delegation of Japan · CCA MUN ’26 · UNGA

**Committee topic:** Deliberation on existing loopholes in the nuclear treaties

**Speaking time:** 75 seconds

**Speech length:** 180 words — calibrated for a 75-second full-force delivery

**Status:** FINAL targeted version · 22 August 2026 · supersedes `mod/mod2.md` for the live MOD

**Targets:** Israel · Russia — real targeting, point-of-order-proof

## SPEECH

[HARD — immediate projection]

Every loophole in the nuclear treaty system is not an accident. It is a decision — made by a State, at somebody's expense.

[COLD INDICTMENT — Israel]

Israel: nearly thirty years after signing the Comprehensive Test-Ban Treaty, it has never ratified it — and has never joined the Non-Proliferation Treaty. No comprehensive safeguards. No declared inventory. No verified environmental baseline. The world does not count Israel's nuclear capability; it estimates it. **ESTIMATES ARE NOT ACCOUNTABILITY.** What the world may not verify, it cannot assess for ecological threat.

[HOTTER — Russia]

Russia: the last binding pillar of United States–Russia arms control expired in February. For the first time in half a century, the two largest arsenals answer to no treaty — no limits, no inspections, no exchanges. In 2023, Moscow revoked its test-ban ratification. **A MORATORIUM IS A POLICY. A RATIFICATION IS A FIREWALL.** A depositary of the NPT now stands outside the test-ban firewall — above the Arctic's heaviest testing legacy.

[CALM STEEL — Japan]

Japan accepts what it demands: full-scope safeguards, published data, independent verification.

[FINAL VERDICT]

Both delegations can explain. This committee demands a standard. **STOP EXPLAINING. START CONTRIBUTING.**

[DROP]

I yield.

## THE NEGATIVE-MARKING SHIELD

Why the chair cannot rule you out of order and why neither target can fact-check you:

1. **No ad hominem.** Every attack targets treaty posture — signature status, ratification status, safeguards status — not the nation, its people or its leadership.
2. **Every number is either neutral-verifiable or explicitly hedged.** “Estimated.” “Nearly thirty years.” “In February.” “In 2023.”
3. **No arsenal counts stated as fact.** The word “estimated” converts the entire Israel passage from assertion to observation.
4. **No allegation of contamination.** You never claim Israel or Russia is polluting. You claim the world has **no verified baseline** — an absence nobody can deny without producing one.
5. **Japan self-inoculates before the close.** You demand nothing you do not accept. That kills the “hypocrite” counter.
6. **The speech ends on a standard, not an insult.** The verdict is constructive — “this committee demands a standard.” Judges score solution-drivers, not attackers.
7. **Both targets get identical structural treatment.** It reads as principled consistency, not a grudge.
8. **The two POC traps to never touch:** (a) “Israel has nuclear weapons” — say capability + estimated; (b) “Russia destroyed New START” — say expired; the indictment is implicit, not asserted.

## REBUTTAL PRE-EMPTS

**If Russia says: “We offered to keep New START limits for a year.”**
> An offer without inspections, without data exchanges and without a signed document is a policy. Policies change with phone calls. Which treaty replaces it — and when?

**If Russia says: “We observe the moratorium; we cooperate with CTBTO.”**
> Then ratification costs nothing. Restore it.

**If Israel says: “Ambiguity protects our security.”**
> Japan never asked Israel to disclose anything about weapons. Ratification of a treaty that bans testing, and environmental monitoring, reveal nothing about weapons. What is being protected — security, or scrutiny?

**If Israel says: “We cooperate with the IAEA.”**
> Membership in the Agency is not comprehensive safeguards. Name one facility the world may verify.

**If either claims: “We are fully transparent.”**
> Transparency is not a statement. It is a structure. Choose one: an environmental baseline, independent monitoring, or a binding treaty.

## FOLLOW-UP POIs

**To Israel**
1. Name one category of environmental data from the Negev Nuclear Research Center that could be published without revealing weapons information.
2. Would Israel accept IAEA environmental sampling requiring no declaration of possession, no inventory and no change to its policy of ambiguity?
3. The CTBT demands no disclosure of designs, no disarmament and no inspections before entry into force. What security interest does ratification itself threaten?

**To Russia**
1. Which binding instrument will replace New START — name the reporting categories, the verification method and the date.
2. A moratorium is a promise. Who verifies a promise? Will Russia restore its CTBT ratification before any new test is even considered?
3. If Russia continues to observe the central limits, why can it not sign a document that makes those limits verifiable?
