# MOD 2 — SOURCE-STATE CONTROL OVER NUCLEAR-LEGACY EVIDENCE

## Delegation of Japan · CCA MUN ’26 · UNGA

**Bloc topic:** Evaluating whether source States should retain exclusive authority to classify, disclose, and assess evidence of transboundary contamination from their nuclear-testing and weapons-production legacies

**Speaking time:** 75 seconds

**Speech length:** 189 words — calibrated for a fast, full-force 75-second delivery

**Status:** Final maximum-aggression, fact-audited version · 21 August 2026

---

## SPEECH

[HARD — immediate challenge]

Who decides whether contamination exists? Too often, the source State classifies the records, selects the model, calculates the dose, and announces the conclusion.

[FULL FORCE]

**THE STATE THAT CREATED THE RISK BECOMES THE WITNESS, THE LABORATORY, AND THE JUDGE.**

[CONTROLLED INDICTMENT]

Japan has already paid. In 1954, all twenty-three Lucky Dragon crew were irradiated outside the declared danger zone. The boundary was an assumption; fallout exposed it.

Kazakhstan’s former Soviet site saw 456 tests. Archives remain essential for dose reconstruction. Yet affected communities are told: prove harm using evidence you cannot see.

[FULL FORCE]

**THAT IS NOT A BURDEN OF PROOF. IT IS A BARRIER TO PROOF.**

[LEGAL AUTHORITY]

UNGA Resolution 79/60 urges testing States to share, as appropriate, technical and scientific information with affected States.

[MECHANISM — clear]

Japan demands three keys: a source-record package; an affected-State counter-assessment; and protected independent technical review.

[CLIPPED]

No unannounced inspections. No weapon designs. No automatic liability.

[CLIMB]

A sound source model survives independent review. Secrecy must not rescue one that cannot.

[FULL FORCE]

**WITHHELD DATA IS NOT SCIENTIFIC UNCERTAINTY. IT IS ENGINEERED UNCERTAINTY.**

A source State receives a voice—not a monopoly over truth.

[FINAL VERDICT]

**CLASSIFICATION CAN PROTECT A SECRET. IT CANNOT MANUFACTURE INNOCENCE.**

[DROP]

I yield.

## POINT-OF-ORDER FIREWALL

**If challenged that Lucky Dragon was not outside the danger area:**

> “Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum expressly records that the vessel was fishing well outside the declared United States danger area and that all twenty-three crew were exposed.”

**If challenged that Japan assigned liability through Lucky Dragon:**

> “Japan stated the verified exposure and failure of a declared boundary. It did not state a legal compensation judgment or name a liable State.”

**If Russia challenges the Kazakhstan language:**

> “Japan assigned no automatic successor liability. It stated the verified 456-test record and the scientific value of archives for retrospective dosimetry.”

**If challenged that UNGA 79/60 does not compel disclosure:**

> “Correct. Japan quoted its actual language: States are urged to share information ‘as appropriate’. Japan’s mechanism operationalizes that voluntary standard with security protection.”

**If accused of violating sovereignty:**

> “Any site access remains subject to territorial-State consent. The source package and protected review do not authorize unannounced inspections.”

**If accused of demanding weapon secrets:**

> “The speech expressly excludes weapon designs. Environmental findings, model assumptions and dose methodology are not automatically weapon blueprints.”

**If challenged on “engineered uncertainty”:**

> “Japan does not infer contamination merely because data are absent. It rejects the circular claim that evidence withheld by the record-holder can itself prove safety. The remedy is review, not a presumption of guilt.”

**If accused of presuming guilt:**

> “The speech expressly excludes automatic liability. Independent review may confirm a source State’s conclusion; the objection is exclusivity, not nationality.”

**If France or the United States says national laboratories are already independent:**

> “Japan keeps the source laboratory inside the review. Technical expertise justifies participation—not exclusive authority over a disputed transboundary conclusion.”

**If attacked on Fukushima:**

> “The motion concerns nuclear-testing and weapons-production legacies. Routine safeguarded civilian activity is outside its defined scope; Japan nevertheless accepts international monitoring in that separate file.”

## FINAL SAFETY RULE

The speech attacks **exclusive authority**, not source-State participation. Japan’s position remains:

> Source States retain a voice and legitimate secrets. Territorial States retain consent. Affected States receive a guaranteed role in the evidence. No single actor controls all three keys.
