## STATUS SUMMARY (the situation as of Aug 21, 2026)

1. **Arsenals rising, not falling** — 12,241 warheads; all nine states modernizing; China fastest (+100/yr; projected 1,000+ by 2030, 1,500 by 2035).
2. **Treaty machinery stalled** — NPT RevCon failed 3× straight; CTBT not in force (8 Annex-2 states + Russia's revoked ratification); New START suspended (2023); TPNW (75 parties) offers a rival path but no nuclear state.
3. **The testing taboo is under direct pressure** — US testing-order rhetoric (Oct 2025, clarified as subcritical); Russia revoked CTBT ratification; DPRK site activity reports.
4. **Ecological threats are live and growing** — Runit Dome cracking (120,000 t of waste; DOE 2024 climate report); Punggye-ri collapse; Central Asia tailings; Arctic dumping; Nature Food famine science; new UN Scientific Panel studying exactly this (report due 2027).
5. **The committee's opportunity** — the ONLY consensus-friendly frame left is ecological: no state can vote against "clean up contamination, monitor radiation, assess before testing." Japan is the only delegation that can own it morally and technically.
