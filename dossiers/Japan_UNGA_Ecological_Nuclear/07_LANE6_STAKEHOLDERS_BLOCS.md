## 6.2 Delegation profiles

### NUCLEAR-WEAPON STATES

**United States** — [LIKELY INFERENCE] from record
- Interests: deterrence credibility, NPT leadership, countering China; sensitive post-Oct 2025 testing-order.
- Votes WITH Japan on "United action" (verified 2024); abstained on Scientific Panel (verified).
- Red lines: nothing that reads as "US must disarm first"; no verification demands on US without reciprocity; avoid direct criticism of the testing order.
- What they need: language "in accordance with national security considerations"; universal, not singled-out, obligations.
- **Approach:** bring in early on CTBT "entry into force" language (long-standing US policy position) and on the Legacy Fund as a voluntary-donor mechanism (US contributed to similar). Never attack; let UK/FR carry NATO framing.

**Russia** — [LIKELY INFERENCE] from record
- Votes NO on Japan's resolution; revoked CTBT ratification (Nov 2023); suspended New START (2023); officially maintains testing moratorium.
- Red lines: no Ukraine-adjacent criticism, no verification of its arsenal, no "aggressor" framing.
- What they need: symmetric obligations; recognition of its disarmament-history claims.
- **Approach:** don't chase votes — isolate. The one ask that costs them nothing publicly: "reaffirm the moratorium" (they claim to observe it). POI: "Does your delegation accept an environmental impact assessment before any future test?" — if they refuse, the room sees it.

**China** — [LIKELY INFERENCE] from record
- Votes NO on Japan's resolution; voted YES on Scientific Panel (verified); fastest-growing arsenal (+100/yr, SIPRI); criticized in Defense of Japan 2026 (verified Aug 4, 2026).
- Red lines: no transparency demands framed as "China-targeted"; any criticism of its buildup will harden its position; supports NWFZ protocols (ratified CANWFZ protocol — verified).
- What they need: anti-US/anti-NATO symmetry; recognition of no-first-use policy.
- **Approach:** use universal language ("all states with nuclear weapons"). Quietly, the ecological frame lets China support monitoring without endorsing US framing. Do NOT name China in draft text (Japan's white paper is for speeches, not clauses).

**United Kingdom / France** — [LIKELY INFERENCE] from record
- UK votes YES on Japan's resolution; both voted AGAINST the Scientific Panel (verified) — their sore spot is new studies/panels; both ratified CTBT; UK ratified CANWFZ protocol.
- Red lines: no language questioning deterrence; no new binding obligations.
- **Approach:** allies on CTBT and remediation; soft-pedal the Scientific Panel angle with them (frame it as "already established — the committee should welcome and feed it, not re-litigate it").

**India / Pakistan** — [LIKELY INFERENCE] from record
- Non-NPT states; abstained on Scientific Panel (verified); abstained on Japan's resolution (India; verified).
- Red lines: no NPT accession pressure; no CTBT ratification pressure in binding language ("calls upon" is fine); Pakistan rejects anything India-first.
- What they need: recognition of their "responsible nuclear state" claims; no singling out.
- **Approach:** the Nature Food 2-billion figure directly implicates India–Pakistan conflict — use it carefully, as "science says even a regional war starves billions," never naming them in text.

**DPRK (if present)** — votes NO; demands recognition as nuclear state; rejects all verification.
- **Approach:** do not engage substantively; let the room's consensus isolate them; the Punggye-ri collapse POI is your weapon if they speak.

**Israel (if present)** — abstains; non-NPT; opacity policy.
- **Approach:** universal language only.

### AFFECTED STATES (your moral core)

**Marshall Islands** — [VERIFIED FACT] 67 tests; ICJ cases 2014–16 (dismissed on jurisdiction); Runit Dome crisis; president's 2016 statement: interest "by virtue of the suffering of its people."
- Wants: remediation commitments, recognition, international attention.
- **Approach:** FIRST recruit. Offer: co-authorship of the Legacy Fund clause + a Runit Dome pilot project named in the resolution. Their moral standing legitimizes the whole draft. Also the Scientific Panel resolution was pushed by Pacific states (Fiji, Kiribati, Palau, Samoa, Tonga sponsored — verified) — they'll join anything that feeds the Panel.

**Kazakhstan** — [VERIFIED FACT] 456 tests; closed site Aug 29, 1991; initiated International Day (2009); CANWFZ leader; ratified TPNW (2019).
- Wants: recognition of Semipalatinsk, NWFZ model promotion, remediation support.
- **Approach:** co-author; offer Semipalatinsk as the second pilot site (Central Asia balance); cite CANWFZ as the model for Japan's monitoring protocol. Kazakhstan is the perfect bridge to the TPNW bloc.

**Pacific islands (Fiji, Palau, Tuvalu, Samoa, Kiribati, Tonga, Vanuatu)** — testing legacy + climate + Fukushima concerns.
- Wants: remediation, monitoring, and (on Fukushima) reassurance/verification.
- **Approach:** the Fukushima tension is real — pre-empt one-on-one with the IAEA evidence; offer them co-authorship of the monitoring clause; their participation turns the discharge debate into a verification success story.

**Australia / New Zealand** — Australia: strong CTBT supporter, US ally; NZ: TPNW party, co-led the Scientific Panel resolution (verified).
- **Approach:** NZ = bridge to TPNW bloc (ask NZ to vouch for Japan's good faith); Australia = CTBT vote anchor.

### SWING / BRIDGE STATES

**Republic of Korea** — US ally, DPRK threat, hosted 2025 Busan summit context; votes with Japan generally.
- **Approach:** natural co-sponsor; share the DPRK-Punggye-ri angle.

**Nordics (Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Iceland)** — humanitarian-initiative core; Sweden ratified TPNW; Norway hosts Oslo conference history.
- **Approach:** bring via "humanitarian and environmental dimensions" language + disarmament education.

**Ireland / Austria / Mexico / South Africa / New Zealand (TPNW core)** — the prohibition bloc.
- Wants: TPNW recognition; strong "catastrophic consequences" language.
- **Approach:** offer recognizing language + capacity-building; warn privately (via NZ/Ireland) that a maximalist rival resolution splits the room and passes nothing — the "bridge" resolution is the only one that becomes reality.

**Brazil / Egypt / Türkiye / Indonesia / G77-NAM core** — [LIKELY INFERENCE] support disarmament, resist anything seen as great-power agenda; want development/technical assistance.
- **Approach:** technical-assistance clause + capacity-building = their price; also "peaceful uses of nuclear energy" reassurance (Art. IV) so non-proliferation pressure isn't read as energy discrimination.

**China/Russia allies (Belarus, Syria, Nicaragua, Iran if present, Cuba, Venezuela)** — likely no votes; Iran has real proliferation-salient issues (2026 RevCon bracketed Iran language — verified).
- **Approach:** don't waste time; one professional engagement each, then ignore.
