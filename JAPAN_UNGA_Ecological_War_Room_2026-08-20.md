## 2. COMMITTEE INTELLIGENCE — UNGA (And Why It Matters)

**What UNGA IS:** Plenary + 6 Main Committees; for this agenda, First Committee (Disarmament & International Security) reports to plenary. Universal membership (193), one-state-one-vote, simple majority for resolutions (important resolutions 2/3), **recommendatory only** — cannot bind, cannot authorize force, cannot impose sanctions or ICJ jurisdiction without separate action. It **can**: request Secretary-General reports, establish subsidiary bodies (the 2025 Scientific Panel is the model [4](https://www.icanw.org/un_approves_new_study_on_effects_of_nuclear_war)), endorse principles (e.g., environmental norms), create voluntary trust funds, and ask UNEP/IAEA/WHO/CTBTO to cooperate. It **cannot**: amend the NPT, force CTBT entry-into-force, create liability tribunals, or override UNCLOS/ITLOS.

**What it is NOT:** UNGA is not UNSC (no veto, no Chapter VII, no sanctions), not UNHRC (no UPR/commission of inquiry), not ICJ, not IAEA Board. **Fantasy trap:** Proposing "UNGA sanctions Japan for ALPS" or "UNGA creates binding compensation fund" dies on mandate. Proposing "UNGA requests SG, in cooperation with IAEA/UNEP/CTBTO, to produce a 2027 assessment and voluntary guidelines, funded by voluntary contributions, reporting back to 81st session" passes.

**Voting structure:** 193 members; you need ~97 votes if all present. Sponsors vs signatories matters on this circuit (sponsors own language; signatories just want debate). Procedural votes (motions) = simple majority of present & voting, no abstention blocking.

**Conference rules (BDBBPS / CCA circuit — derived from your uploaded WP 1.2):** 29 delegations across nine regions in that UNGA, 21 operative clauses, simple majority, working paper → draft resolution → amendments → voting. No crisis backroom. Assume standard GSL + moderated/unmoderated caucuses, points, motions. **Check your ROP for yielding and right-of-reply — we assume UNA-USA style.**
