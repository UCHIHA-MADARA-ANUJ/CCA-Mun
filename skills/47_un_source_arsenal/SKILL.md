## Embedded Core Enforcement

This skill is self-contained. Apply these rules before its specialist workflow:

- Verify any current, changing, contested, legal, numerical, voting, or country-position claim using authoritative sources when tools are available.
- Prefer official UN documents, official government or permanent-mission statements, treaty texts, and official international organisations.
- Never invent facts, quotations, statistics, resolution numbers, votes, legal authority, sources, dates, or country positions.
- Label uncertainty as `[VERIFIED FACT]`, `[LIKELY INFERENCE]`, `[NEGOTIATION STRATEGY]`, or `[UNVERIFIED — DO NOT USE IN COMMITTEE]`.
- Keep all recommendations consistent with the assigned country, committee mandate, and simulation date.
- Challenge policy weaknesses—evidence, authority, funding, implementation, safeguards, and contradictions—not people or countries personally.
- For every serious proposal identify the implementing actor, authority, resources, timeline, monitoring, and safeguards.
- Turn research into a usable speech, question, negotiation move, clause, or defense.

# UN SOURCE ARSENAL — OPERATING PROTOCOL

## Mission

End the "where do I even look" problem. Every MUN claim class has a home base; this skill maps the arsenal and the search tactics so evidence gathering is minutes, not hours. Always inspect the actual document — never cite a snippet.

## 1. The UN Document System (learn the symbols)

UN documents are identified by symbols. Knowing the symbol is the fastest path to the full text:

- **A/RES/** — General Assembly resolutions (e.g., A/RES/70/1 — the 2030 Agenda).
- **S/RES/** — Security Council resolutions (e.g., S/RES/1325).
- **E/RES/** — ECOSOC resolutions (e.g., E/RES/2020/4).
- **A/HRC/RES/** — Human Rights Council resolutions.
- **A/CONF./** — conference documents; **A/70/251** style — session documents; **S/PV.** — Security Council meeting records; **A/70/PV.** — GA meeting records.

**Lookup:** paste the symbol after `https://undocs.org/` to open the full text (e.g., undocs.org/A/RES/70/1).

## 2. The Core UN Portals

| Need | Go to |
|---|---|
| Resolutions, decisions, voting data, sponsors, draft resolutions, speeches, maps | UN Digital Library — digitallibrary.un.org (search by topic, organ, document type; advanced search finds sponsors and drafts; linked data connects resolution → vote → meeting record) |
| Full text by symbol | undocs.org (official document system; add the symbol) |
| How to find UN documents (official guides) | research.un.org (Dag Hammarskjöld Library research guides: Find Resolutions, Find Reports, etc.) |
| Old documents (pre-1993 full text) | UN ODS — documents.un.org |
| Statements and speeches | UN Web TV — webtv.un.org; meeting records via Digital Library |
| Statistics and indicators | UNdata — data.un.org (aggregates UN statistical databases) |
| Treaties and ratifications | UN Treaty Collection — treaties.un.org |
| Member states, charter, structure | un.org; UN Charter text; UN Member States pages |
| Voting records and explanations of vote | Digital Library voting records; meeting records; permanent mission statements |

## 3. Intergovernmental Data (per topic family)

- **Health:** WHO Global Health Observatory (who.int/data); WHO reports.
- **Refugees/migration:** UNHCR data portal and Global Trends reports; IOM reports.
- **Children/education:** UNICEF data and State of the World's Children; UNESCO statistics.
- **Food/hunger:** WFP; FAO (FAOSTAT); IFAD.
- **Labor:** ILO databases (ILOSTAT) and reports.
- **Development/economy:** World Bank Open Data (data.worldbank.org) and World Development Indicators; IMF (imf.org — World Economic Outlook, Article IV reports).
- **Trade:** WTO statistics portal; UNCTAD data; OEC (oec.world) for trade flows.
- **Climate/environment:** IPCC reports; UNEP; UNFCCC (climate pledges, NDCs).
- **Nuclear:** IAEA (safety, safeguards, energy statistics).
- **Disarmament/arms:** SIPRI (sipri.org — arms transfers, military expenditure); UNODA; IISS Military Balance.
- **Crime/drugs:** UNODC (World Drug Report, homicide stats).
- **Human rights:** OHCHR (treaty body documents, UPR outcomes, special procedures); Universal Human Rights Index.
- **Gender:** UN Women; UNFPA.
- **Humanitarian:** OCHA (reliefweb.int — field reports, funding); WFP.
- **Law/justice:** ICJ (icj-cij.org — judgments and advisory opinions); ICC (icc-cpi.int); ICRC (IHL treaties/customary law).

## 4. National and Government Sources

- **Foreign ministries** — official position papers, statements, national strategies.
- **Permanent missions to the UN** — statements in debates, explanations of vote, national positions (mission websites, often under "statements").
- **Embassies** — policy pages, joint statements.
- **National statistical offices** — authoritative national numbers (often disagree with UN estimates — report the difference).
- **Parliaments/governments** — laws, ratified treaties, official policy documents.

## 5. Analysis and Expert Sources (context and interpretation)

- **Think tanks:** Chatham House, Brookings, CSIS, RAND, Carnegie Endowment, Council on Foreign Relations, ODI, Atlantic Council, Stimson, CIGI.
- **Academic:** Google Scholar; peer-reviewed journals; university research centers.
- **News:** wires and established broadcasters only to confirm developing events, never as the primary foundation of a claim (see Core Protocol source tiers).

## 6. Search Tactics That Save Hours

- `site:un.org "country name" agenda item` — official UN pages only.
- `site:un.org "A/RES/70/1"` — find pages referencing a resolution.
- `"explanation of vote" "Country X"` — find official position statements on a vote.
- `"S/RES/XXXX" filetype:pdf` — get the PDF directly.
- `"Minister of Foreign Affairs of X" statement "General Debate"` — GA general debate speeches (September) are gold for current positions.
- In the UN Digital Library: filter by organ, document type, and date; check "related documents" for the draft resolution, sponsors, and vote.
- For historical committees: period government documents, treaty collections, UN yearbooks, and archives (national archives; UN Audiovisual Library; period news via newspaper archives).
- Wayback Machine (web.archive.org) for recovering removed official pages.

## 7. Verification Traps

- **NGO factoids presented as official statistics** — verify with the primary agency.
- **Outdated numbers** — check the reporting year and population scope.
- **Misattributed resolutions** — confirm the symbol and the organ (GA vs SC matters: GA = recommendation, SC can bind).
- **Confusing binding and non-binding** — a "demands" clause in a GA resolution is still a recommendation in law.
- **Quote without context** — verify speaker, venue, date, and full sentence.

## 8. Source Lookup by Claim Type (rapid)

| Claim type | First target |
|---|---|
| Country stance | Foreign ministry / permanent mission / explanation of vote |
| UN action | UN Digital Library (resolution + vote + sponsors) |
| Treaty status | UN Treaty Collection |
| Statistic | UNdata / relevant agency (WHO, World Bank, UNHCR…) |
| Current event | UN press releases / OCHA reliefweb / reputable news (confirm) |
| Historical fact | Period documents, UN Yearbook, archives |
| Legal status | ICJ / OHCHR / treaty text |

## 9. Red-Team Quality Gate

1. Is each central claim traceable to a named source with date and scope?
2. Is the source the most authoritative one that exists for that claim class?
3. Could an opponent challenge the source's date, methodology, or legal status — and is the delegate ready with the primary document?
