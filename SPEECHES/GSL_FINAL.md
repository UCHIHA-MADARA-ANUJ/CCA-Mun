## THE FINAL SPEECH (memorize structure, not syllables)

🐢 The sky over Hiroshima caught fire. Nine days later — Nagasaki.
And **fifteen days ago**, on their anniversary, Japan stood silent again… counting how little has changed.

Honourable Chair, distinguished delegates —

⚡ Look at this record. The last arms-control treaty between the great powers **expired in February. No successor. No talks.** The largest nuclear plant in Europe survives tonight on **one power line.** The fastest warhead buildup since the Cold War proceeds **behind closed doors.**
✊ And when this Assembly was asked to help the poisoned… **four flags voted no. Three years running.**
[BEAT] 🐢 *They know who they are.*

Japan knows this file longer than any delegation in this room. **Thirty years of our resolutions on this floor.** The world's only decade of post-accident cleanup. ⚡ A Nobel Peace Prize earned by our survivors.

So Japan did not come to mourn. Japan came with **machinery.** Ten nations — every continent — stand in the **ECO-NEXUS** around three instruments: 🐢 *shared monitoring. early-warning across borders. real remediation of poisoned land.*

✊ 🐢 Hiroshima was the first city destroyed by the bomb. Whether Nagasaki remains the **last**… depends, as Japan's Prime Minister told this very Assembly, on *the wisdom of humankind.*
[BEAT] **This room is where that wisdom answers.**

In ten days, the real United Nations convenes on exactly this file. **We write its first chapter — here.**

Radiation never needed a visa. ✊ Neither does responsibility.

Japan came to build.

🐢 **Who builds with us?**

## 🧬 WHY THIS IS THE FINAL (design logic, learn it — chairs grade these instincts)

1. **Arc = grief → evidence → credentials → product → destiny → challenge.** A GSL must travel from emotion to action; every word services the next.
2. **The Ishiba line is the hinge.** "This very Assembly" + "this room" collapses the distance between the real UN and your committee — you make a school hall feel like New York. That's the single highest-status move available in 90 seconds.
3. **"Japan did not come to mourn. Japan came with machinery."** — the thesis. Memory establishes the right to speak; machinery establishes the right to LEAD. Chairs award leaders, not mourners.
4. **"They know who they are" stays un-named** — the four NO-flags self-identify by silence or by flinching. Aggression with perfect manners.
5. **"Who builds with us?"** = recruitment inside rhetoric: your 10 flags feel claimed on stage; undecideds feel the train leaving.

## 🧪 DELIVERY DRILLS (run these, bro)
- **Drill 1 (pace):** read at TV-news speed. If under 85s → you're rushing; slow the 🐢 lines.
- **Drill 2 (the hinge):** say the Nagasaki line 10× until "the wisdom of humankind" sounds like a verdict, not a quote.
- **Drill 3 (eyes):** "They know who they are" — look at the dais, NOT at those delegates. (Dignity.) "Who builds with us?" — sweep the whole room, land ON undecideds.
