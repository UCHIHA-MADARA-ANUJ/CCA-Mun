## THE SPEECH

🐢 Eighty-one years ago this month, the sky over Hiroshima caught fire.
Nine days later… Nagasaki.
And **fifteen days ago**, on their anniversary, Japan stood silent again — counting how little has changed.

[BEAT] Honourable Chair, distinguished delegates — look at this record:

⚡ The last arms-control treaty between the great powers expired in February. **No successor. No talks.**
A nuclear power plant — the largest in Europe — survives tonight on **a single power line.**
The fastest warhead buildup since the Cold War proceeds behind closed doors.
A state declares its arsenal — *their word* — **"irreversible."**
✊ And when this Assembly was asked to help the poisoned and the irradiated… **four flags voted no. Three years running.** [BEAT] 🐢 They know who they are.

Enough.

The Delegation of Japan stopped asking for permission. **Ten nations — every continent on this map** — stand in the ECO-NEXUS to build the machine this century owes the poisoned: ⚡ *monitor together. Warn each other. Clean what was burned.*

In ten days, the real United Nations convenes on exactly this file. **This committee can write its first chapter.**

🐢 Radiation never needed a visa. [BEAT] Neither does responsibility.

Japan came to build.

✊ **Who builds with us?**
