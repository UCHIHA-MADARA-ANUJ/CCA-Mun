## THE DRAFT (read aloud & time yourself — target 1:25–1:30)

Honorable Chair, distinguished delegates.

🐢 Fifteen days ago… my country stood silent.

On August 6th — and again on August 9th — Japan marked **eighty-one years** since the sky opened over Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Two hundred thousand lives… became a statistic this institution still hasn't learned from.

[BEAT] ✊ I said *institution*. **Not accident.**

Because look at the record. ⚡ Three Review Conferences — collapsed. The last arms-control treaty between the great powers — **expired in February. Nobody flinched.** While this room debated doctrine, a nuclear plant in Europe survived on **one power line**, and poisoned land from Semipalatinsk to the Pacific kept quietly billing the innocent.

[BEAT] 🐢 Radiation doesn't need a visa. It never did.

So Japan stopped asking permission. Ten nations — every continent on this map — now stand in the **ECO-NEXUS**, with one conviction: we will not wait for the perfect treaty. ⚡ We will build the machine — **monitor together, warn each other, clean what was poisoned.**

✊ Some of you will call it modest.
[BEAT] 🐢 Then show me your better one.

In ten days, the real United Nations convenes on *exactly this* — victim assistance, environmental remediation, September the 1st. **We** write its first chapter — here, this weekend.

🐢 Japan came to build.

✊ **Who builds with us?**
