## MOVE CARDS (top 8 in full)

### M1 — Scope-Definition Opening (the dictionary heist)
- **Upside:** Owns every subsequent clause's terms of debate; the single highest-leverage 90 seconds available.
- **Resources:** pre-written speech (war brief §13.2) + the four-line inoculation insert (below).
- **Difficulty:** Low (prepared). **Time-to-impact:** immediate. **Reversibility:** none needed.
- **Competitor response:** PRC reframe attempt within 1–2 speeches.
- **Risk:** chair cuts time; over-emoting. Mitigation: 60-sec compression ready.
- **Dependencies:** early speakers'-list position (note chair at roll call).
- **Metric:** by lunch — delegates using OUR nouns ("assessment," "weapons enterprise," "legacy sites") unprompted. **If absent by lunch → trigger T1.**
- **Inoculation insert (verbatim, place after Fukushima mention):** *"This very week, independent IAEA sampling accompanies every batch Japan releases — twenty-one consecutive batches verified far below operational limits. Transparency is not a favor we grant; it is infrastructure we build."*

### M2 — Coalition Founding Dash (first 90 seconds of unmod #1)
- **Upside:** Locks moral-frontman custody + September co-brand + first-sponsor anchor simultaneously.
- **Script:** *"Your resolution built this agenda. Our machinery can fund it. September's meeting needs a framework — let's write it together, your names first on the victim-assistance pillar."*
- **Competitor response:** PRC counters with PIF-side offers later; abolition core may shop our offer to Switzerland-type. Counter: it's already signed in the room's memory — public timestamp at caucus end.
- **Risk:** their delegates squirrel (want leadership). Fallback: pillar custody + named co-sponsor + their language in PP (they almost always take this).
- **Metric:** 3 legacy-state sponsors committed before unmod ends. **If 0 → trigger T3 (Plan H).**

### M3 — Neutral-Bridge Absorption
- **Upside:** Deletes our substitutability threat (file 02/D-tier2).
- **Offer:** verification-annex lead (OP9) + methodology naming.
- **Response-model:** if refused and they file anyway → do NOT compete; convert to merger target with annex custody swap.
- **Metric:** annex lead accepted day 1.

### M4 — Abolition Merger-Price Pre-Quote
- **Content:** preamble acknowledgement ("noting the Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons' entry into force" — noting ≠ endorsing; standard UN verbs) + humanitarian pillar custody + first-billed moral speakers.
- **Why first-mover pricing matters:** whoever quotes the price frame first anchors it. If they open with "TPNW endorsement or nothing," we're negotiating uphill for hours.
- **Risk:** leakage to PRC that we're "courting purity camp" → neutralized by parallel M7 (USA pre-registration) the same afternoon; symmetry is the story.
- **Metric:** handshake architecture EOD1.

### M5 — The 90-Minute Filing
- **Specification:** war brief §15 architecture, max 3 pages, 8-10 PPs, 10 OPs, UN-verb discipline (requests/invites/calls upon/encourages), sponsor block spans 5 caucus-groups.
- **Difficulty:** Moderate — the content is pre-built; only assembly remains.
- **Risk vector:** chair-side WP rules (some circuits require N sponsors before acceptance) → ask ROP at first break. **UNKNOWN until then → see file 11.**
- **Metric:** WP number assigned by session 2; referenced by other delegates by session 3.

### M6 — PIF Custody Pack
- **Contents:** OP6 named-co-sponsor offer; one pre-drafted 2-line amendment they can own ("regional monitoring participation with capacity support"); quiet promise: we never litigate ALPS with them present.
- **Response-model:** PRC counter-offer likely louder (public solidarity declaration). Counter: ours is *text-custody*, theirs is *praise*. Delegates bring text home.
- **Metric:** ≥2 PIF delegates co-sign OP6 by day-2 morning.

### M7 — USA Pre-Registration (the abstention-engineering move)
- **Content:** share soft-OP3 early ("maintain moratoria" + IMS completion, no dates, no names); ask nothing publicly.
- **Psychology:** the delegate learns OUR text is the only one s/he can touch without breaking roleplay. Purchased with a preview — zero concessions.
- **Risk:** they leak to PRC for leverage → acceptable; the clause is already tolerance-priced.
- **Metric:** US delegate no-opposition signal before OP3 debate.

### M12 — Closing Speech (adoption-claim architecture)
- **Structure:** journey-narration of the text (who gave what) → September deadline → refrain.
- **Purpose:** writes the record chairs score: *the document exists because this delegation ran it.*
- **Metric:** chair's closing remarks reference the process; rival delegates thank us in theirs.

## 🌲 COUNTERMOVE TREES (the one-move → three-move stacks)

### TREE A: We open with scope definition (M1)
- **PRC response 1:** moderated-caucus motion on "ongoing radioactive releases." 
  → **Our counter:** speak IN their caucus — first 10s agree contamination is ongoing concern; rest frames universal verification architecture; motion's energy absorbed.
  → **Their counter-counter:** motion a *speaker-list vote* on ALPS-specific language. 
  → **End state:** we object-once-then-go-silent; if such motion somehow carries, we execute season-neutral clause participation (it's universal, remember) — and their moment produces OUR clause's first co-sponsors. **Outcome: acceptable either way if we never appear rattled.**
- **PRC response 2:** ignore scope, attack conduct directly in GSL.
  → our counter: single-defense line + pivot; **their C-C:** repetition (risks looking obsessed to floaters — self-damaging; let it run).

### TREE B: We file WP first (M5)
- **Abolition response:** file parallel moral WP by session 3.
  → **Our counter:** public welcome + merger invitation on quoted price (M4).
  → **Their C-C:** attempt sponsorship race (count co-signers aloud).
  → **End state:** race conceded gracefully ("this committee has room for every commitment; the Coalition's doors stay open") — then numbers matter less at merger than verbs; we hold the machinery either way. **Posture: serene.**
- **PRC-side response:** hostile WP with liability verbs.
  → universal-application counter-amendment prewritten (war brief §15.4); **their C-C:** accuse dilution; **end state:** they defend Lop Nur-adjacent carve-outs or withdraw — both acceptable.

### TREE C: Merger negotiation day-2 (M10)
- **Rival demand:** TPNW-fidelity OP.
  → **Response:** offer *preamble* upgrade instead; if demanded OP survives into floor, request division of question per ROP; if lost, vote against OP-with-our-name-detached-script (preplanned: "Japan's assent to the architecture stands; our national position on OP_ remains of record").
  → **Their C-C:** threaten walk-out. **End state:** they never walk from a live September-meeting linkage. Hold.
- **P3-side demand:** delete OP4.
  → offer verb-downgrade ("affirms"→"recalls the applicability of"). If they want deletion anyway, accept clause-as-recommendation-to-IAEA (venue-shift, substance preserved). **Reversible trades only.**

### TREE D: The amendment-hour ALPS ambush
- **Move:** "independent sampling by affected states" amendment appears.
  → **Response:** friendly-amendment substitute: "IAEA-facilitated expanded participation including affected-state experts" (our 2024 reality, universalized).
  → **Their counter:** insist on non-IAEA frame.
  → **End state:** they must publicly prefer surveillance-politics to the existing verified framework; POI closes: "Does the amendment's sponsor find the IAEA's on-site, expert-inclusive sampling of twenty-one batches insufficient — and by what technical standard?" **Withdrawal or defeat both count as wins.**
