# 18 — POI PACK (Points of Information — interrogation ammunition)
**Doctrine:** A POI is not a question; it's a loaded trap with a flag on it. Every POI = short, factual, and forces the target to either (a) concede our frame, or (b) look evasive in front of the chair. Ask one, sit down, let silence work. Never stack three.

---

## A. HOSTILE POIS

### 🎯 vs CHINA (the ALPS question — they WILL raise it; beat them to the frame)
1. *"Does the delegate of China agree that assessments of transboundary radiological impact should be based on IAEA-monitored data — the same monitoring arrangements in which Chinese experts participate?"*
 — Trap: yes = our OP2/S1 standard locked, ALPS line dead; no = China attacks the IAEA on record. [FACT base: expert participation under Sep 2024 arrangement; 21 batches below limits]
2. *"Can China name a single IAEA finding that Fukushima's discharged water exceeded operational limits in any of the 21 completed batches?"* — (There isn't one. Silence is the point.)
3. *"China abstained on resolutions 78/240, 79/60 and 80/56 — victims' assistance. Is the delegation's concern for ecological harm selectively applied to geography?"* — uses E32 facts; careful: aggressive, use once, late game.
4. **If China attacks first, floor counter (not POI):** *"Japan welcomes this committee's attention to data. Then let us agree: IAEA-verified data governs. Japan's entire discharge record — every batch — is published under it. We invite China's experts, as we have since 2024. Delegations who trust science do not fear this clause. Question is who does."*

### 🎯 vs USA
5. *"The United States called for trilateral arms-control engagement in Japan's 2024 text, then abstained in 2025 over gender and SDG riders. Can the delegate confirm the US supports the incremental, verifiable approach itself — so we can count its voice for the machinery in this paper?"* — FACT base: US EOV 31 Oct 2025. Forces yes-or-hypocrisy.
6. *"With New START lapsed since February 2026 and no successor, does the US agree monitoring infrastructure matters MORE, not less, in a treaty vacuum?"* — pure setup for our L1 layer.

### 🎯 vs RUSSIA (if present/shadow presence via allies)
7. *"Does Russia support the IAEA's seven pillars at Zaporizhzhia — including reliable external power — given the plant has survived on a single line since February?"* — FACT: Reuters 19 Feb 2026; IAEA update.

### 🎯 vs FRANCE / UK
8. *"France voted against victim assistance and environmental remediation three consecutive years. Will the delegate at least support the *monitoring* clauses, or is the objection to measuring harm as well as remedying it?"* — E32 receipts. Brutal; deploy only if they move against OP8.

## B. FRIENDLY Lobs (ask allies to let them shine — pre-scripted in bloc chat)
9. → **KZ:** *"Can Kazakhstan brief the committee on what the 1 September meeting needs from this resolution?"* (custody moment)
10. → **Canada:** *"Can Canada share what the committee can learn from its consent-based repository process — launched just this year?"*
11. → **India:** *"How does India see capacity-building closing the monitoring gap for developing states?"* (GCNEP moment)
12. → **Mexico:** *"Mexico stewarded the humanitarian initiative — how does testimony translate into machinery?"*
13. → **Germany:** *"What does a decade of phase-out monitoring teach us about decommissioning data?"*

## C. CHAIR-SMART DISCIPLINE
- One POI per speaker, max. Follow-ups only when the answer is evasive AND the room noticed.
- Never POI your own investigation targets twice in a row — rotate: China hits us → we POI once, then a friendly lob to reset tone, THEN next dagger. Rhythm reads as statesmanship.
- Every hostile POI above is indexed to the evidence log (E-entries). If it's not in the log, it doesn't leave your mouth.
