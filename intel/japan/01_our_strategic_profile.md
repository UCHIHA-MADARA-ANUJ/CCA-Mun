# 01 — OUR STRATEGIC PROFILE: JAPAN 🇯🇵
### The audit: what we claim vs what the evidence shows vs what the room will believe

> Method (per master prompt §1): every asset/liability is split five ways — **CLAIM / EVIDENCE / STAKEHOLDER BELIEF / COMPETITOR BELIEF / UNKNOWN**. No internal claim is accepted unverified.

---

## 1. THE ONLY-A-BOMBED-NATION BRAND

- **WE CLAIM:** Unique moral authority; the natural conscience of nuclear ecology.
- **EVIDENCE SHOWS:** Authority is real but **franchise-shared now**: hibakusha confederation owns the 2024 Nobel; Kazakhstan/Kiribati own the UNGA legacy-file (3 straight resolutions, 161→174→170 votes); ICAN/TPNW own the activist register. Our brand is *seniority*, not monopoly. **FACT**
- **ROOM WILL BELIEVE:** Mixed. NAM + legacy states respect it; abolition core treats us as the conscience that betrayed itself (umbrella + TPNW refusal). **INFERENCE**
- **COMPETITORS BELIEVE:** It's exploitable — a story that peaks in the opening speech and expires on first contradiction. **INFERENCE**
- **UNKNOWN:** How much this specific school circuit's EB weights "authentic country voice" vs "clause engineering." → Probe in opening session via chair's feedback cadence.
- **SO WHAT:** Use the brand as *keynote*, never as *argument*. Facts of experience open doors; only machinery wins votes.

## 2. THE RESOLUTION FRANCHISE (our actual product line)

- **WE CLAIM:** Thirty-two consecutive adopted texts = unshakable convening power.
- **EVIDENCE SHOWS:** Franchise intact but margin eroding: 2021: 158-4-27; 2023: 148-7-29 (plenary); **2025: 145-5 with US ABSTENTION** (first P3 defection). The 2025 text gained a new feature — call for **US-Russia-China trilateral arms-control talks** post-New START expiry — which *cost* the US vote. **FACT** (09_evidence_log §J1)
- **ROOM WILL BELIEVE:** "Japan gets resolutions passed" — unless a PRC/abolition delegate quotes the US-abstention story to paint us as losing our patron. **INFERENCE**
- **COMPETITORS BELIEVE:** The franchise is strong on paper, weak at the top; attacking the alliance gap rattles the delegate playing Japan. **INFERENCE**
- **UNKNOWN:** Whether any rival delegate in *this* room has done the homework to know about the 2025 abstention. Assume yes for the top 3 rivals (likely USA/PRC/abolition delegates) — they're the comprehension set we must pre-frame.
- **SO WHAT:** We declassify it ourselves. "Japan's 2025 resolution called for trilateral talks among the three largest arsenals — the first time we asked our own ally to sit at that table. Sending a text to P5 desks is harder than collecting signatures." Convert the crack into evidence of independence.

## 3. THE UMBRELLA (US extended deterrence) → portfolio dependency

- **WE CLAIM:** Deterrence is defensive reality; Three Non-Nuclear Principles keep identity intact.
- **EVIDENCE SHOWS:** Both true *as domestic policy*, but the dependency constrains movement: 2017 TPNW boycott; NO-votes on TPNW annual resolutions (121-44-12 in 2025); Ban Monitor classifies us "umbrella state." **FACT**
- **ROOM WILL BELIEVE:** The clean abstraction ("structures of deterrence") dissolves when TPNW bloc asks: would Japan sign tomorrow if the US nodded? The honest answer is "we won't speculate" — which everyone translates as *no*. **INFERENCE**
- **COMPETITORS BELIEVE:** This is our most stable weakness — it survives every caucus, every speech, every friendship. They'll store it for voting-phase ambush POIs, not early debate. **INFERENCE — counter: burn it early on our terms (see 04 §SEQUENCE).**
- **SO WHAT:** Never open flank voluntarily; have the 22-second answer so rehearsed it lands with zero emotional leakage. Panic on this question = the clip they replay at lunch.

## 4. FUKUSHIMA: EXPERIENCE ASSET vs DISCHARGE LIABILITY

- **WE CLAIM:** Fukushima = the world's deepest nuclear-safety learning curve + transparency gold standard.
- **EVIDENCE SHOWS:** Both halves true simultaneously. Asset: independent sampling of every batch (21/21 below limits), IAEA office on-site since 2023, expanded framework with PRC participation (Sept 2024). Liability: **batch 22 is discharging 12–17 Aug 2026 — live during the simulation window**; opposition framing ("1.3 million tonnes! 30 years!") needs only headlines, not chemistry. **FACT**
- **ROOM WILL BELIEVE:** Whatever the *first confident speaker* says about it. This is a first-mover narrative vulnerability. **INFERENCE — drives our early-scope move.**
- **UNKNOWN:** Whether the committee dates overlap batch 22 (ends 17 Aug). If yes: threat level max; if weekend later: merely high.
- **SO WHAT:** Defense must convert to *demonstration of our core thesis* — "independent verification converts suspicion into science" — within 25 seconds, every time, verbatim-consistently.

## 5. CAPABILITY PORTFOLIO (the hard assets rivals lack)

- **EVIDENCE:** Hosts CTBTO radionuclide stations (Takasaki/Okinawa); IAEA top-tier contributor; runs the annual UNGA disarmament text; funds UNIDIR/disarmament education; world's most-implemented post-accident monitoring regime; launched plutonium-management transparency reporting (1997 policy → annual INFCIRC publication of stocks). **FACT**
- **SO WHAT:** In a *machinery* contest we hold cards nobody else can even name. This is why strategy must pull the room toward machinery and away from purity.

## 6. ENERGY-SECURITY DEPENDENCY (category-collapse exposure)

- **EVIDENCE:** Kashiwazaki-Kariwa 6 restarted 9 Feb 2026; 7th Basic Energy Plan targets nuclear ~20% by 2040 (≈8-9 GW KK capacity context); Takaichi cabinet explicitly pro-restart. **FACT**
- **RISK:** If scope creeps to "nuclear = ecological threat, full stop," our own energy policy enters the charge sheet. **INFERENCE**
- **SO WHAT:** Agenda-discipline is a core security interest: **OP7-style category firewall is non-negotiable.** Every speech reinforces "weapons enterprise ≠ civilian power under safeguards."

## 7. TEAM / RESOURCES / CONSTRAINTS (the delegate layer)

- **RESOURCES:** This hub + the war-room brief + 41-skill OS. Prepared-state advantage is real but decaying (one good unmoderated = rivals copy the *shape* of our game).
- **CONSTRAINTS (be honest):** one-human bandwidth; no co-delegate redundancy assumed (adjust if two-seat delegation); award criteria unwritten; ROP variant unknown (Indian circuits often run UNA-USA-flavored hybrids).
- **DEPENDENCY:** Our entire plan presumes *speed of filing* and *caucus sequencing*. A slow chair who delays unmoderateds compresses our advantage; a loose one expands it. **UNKNOWN until session 1.**

## 8. POSITIONING STATEMENT (the internal north star — say it, don't speechify it)

> *Japan: the delegation that turns the atom's worst lessons into the room's most workable machinery — the assessor, the funder, the bridge that carries text where no one else can.*

Every intervention checked against it: does this line make us the *assessor* (credibility), the *funder* (capability), the *bridge* (custody of passable text)? If none, cut it.
