## PART B — FIVE-VECTOR STRATEGY

### 🔥 ATTACK (where we gain ground)
1. **The vacant safety lane** — OP4 facility-protection is claimable without firing a shot (Russia can't visibly occupy it, PRC can't sponsor it, abolition camp doesn't care). *Ground gained: one full clause-ownership + chair-credibility.*
2. **The September-meeting input framework** — first GA text to commend principles; permanent association. *Ground gained: legacy beyond the weekend.*
3. **The auditor role** — public FAMMS-fixes on rival texts. *Ground gained: chair-channel + competence aura.*
4. **Scope attack** (counter-intuitive: attack *our own* vulnerabilities first) — self-disclose ALPS + abstention in opening. *Ground gained: hostile ammunition rendered inert.*

### 🛡️ DEFENSE (protect at all costs)
1. **Category firewall** (peaceful uses ≠ weapons enterprise) — AMEND → reject any scope-creep amendment tolerantly and verbally: "conflation serves no affected community."
2. **Chassis custody** — if a rival WP leads, merge INTO it only with OP custody retained; never merge into purity-test text.
3. **P3-tolerable verbs** — every OP reviewed for name-free, consent-based language before circulation.
4. **Speak-in-rotation** — brand dilution is self-inflicted.

### 🔄 COUNTERATTACK (loaded, on trigger)
1. **Against ALPS reframe:** elevation move — "universal IAEA-anchored frameworks for *all* states, all facilities" + one verified datum (21/21 batches; Chinese experts sampled). Never two speeches in a row.
2. **Against TPNW litmus:** 22-second script (appended to file 08) → hand abolition leaders floor-credit → pivot.
3. **Against scope creep:** category discipline + offer the creep a procedural exit ("the distinguished delegate's concern belongs to [IAEA/UNECE-venue] — this committee's leverage is here").
4. **Against our US-abstention being quoted:** the W2 independence flip, once, verbatim-drilled.

### 🌊 FLANK (win without contact)
1. **PIF custody play** — first bilaterals, clause co-ownership, mini-amendment gifts.
2. **NWFZ regional annex nod** (Tlatelolco/Rarotonga/Bangkok/Pelindaba/Semipalatinsk mention-cluster) — costs us one preambular line, buys five-region goodwill.
3. **Institutional-observer friendship** (if IAEA/UNEP role seated) — technical-witness validation of our framework.
4. **Youth/education clause sponsorship** — soft-power clause abolition types love, costs nothing, buys co-sponsors cheaply.

### 🚫 DENIAL (moves that reduce their ability to compete)
1. **Absorb the bridge rivals** (Australia/Swiss-type) into annex leads — removes substitute-product from market.
2. **Close abolition merger-price early** — PRC's only coalition path to scale is through abolition mass; a closed merger = locked door.
3. **Publicly set the filing clock** — "the Coalition circulates its architecture this session" — forces rivals to react to our tempo (whoever sets the calendar owns the room's metabolism).
4. **Every verification demand met with universal-application counter** — denies PRC the wedge between "Japan-specific" and "global."
