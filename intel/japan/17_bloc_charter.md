## 1. THE CHARTER (canonical)

**VANGUARD — Voluntary Alliance for Nuclear Oversight, Global Accountability, and Remediation Directives (VANGUARD)**

**Mission (one line):** *After three failed NPT Review Conferences, somebody has to write the machinery the process couldn't. That's us.*

**The machine — three layers:**
- 🛰️ **MONITOR** — harmonized radiation-monitoring standards + open data platform
- 🚨 **RESPOND** — transboundary early-warning, emergency response, capacity-building
- ♻️ **REMEDIATE** — waste management, decommissioning monitoring, legacy-site remediation, victim assistance, facility protection

**Charter rules — every member accedes to all five:**
1. **No doctrine tests.** The bloc takes no position on any member's nuclear policy. Disarmament positions remain sovereign.
2. **No treaty-name fights.** Text references "relevant international legal instruments"; the sole permitted naming is preambular, with the standard qualifier "for its States parties" (applies equally to NPT, TPNW, CTBT).
3. **Clauses, not flags.** Membership is fixed at founding; no member vets another member; nobody litigates anyone's seat. All conflict is about text.
4. **Drafting discipline.** Core (7) drafts; ring amends through core; no freelance floor amendments from members — ever. Disputes settle inside the bloc chat, never on the floor.
5. **Consultation channel.** Non-member states route concerns/clauses to the core; they get first hearing. (This is how we keep the USA and China quiet without giving them the pen.)

**Structure:** Drafting core = Japan, India, Mexico, Canada, Germany, Kazakhstan, South Korea. Outer ring = Philippines, Iran, Switzerland, South Africa, Brazil/Egypt (cap 13 total).
**Custody map:** per file 16 §5 — Japan holds umbrella clause + preamble arrangement, always.
