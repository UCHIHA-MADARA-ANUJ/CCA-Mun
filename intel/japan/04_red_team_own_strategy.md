## R1. ASSUMPTION-BY-ASSASSINATION (each core assumption shot, then body-searched)

### A1. "The assessment/machinery frame is winnable terrain."
- **Shot:** What if chairs and delegates simply find machinery BORING? School awards skew charismatic. A data-less fire & brimstone delegate can take best-del on vibe. **HYPOTHESIS — plausible.**
- **Disconfirming evidence search:** Our circuit data is thin → **UNKNOWN.** Real-UN contrast: the 2023-25 legacy resolutions (machinery-ish texts!) won 161/174/170 votes *because* they were actionable, not rhetorical. Same psychology applies in rooms: people vote for what maps to movement. **INFERENCE: frame must wear emotion as clothing** — testimony opens, machinery closes. Adjusted, not abandoned.
- **Kill condition:** If after our opening speech + first mod-caucus intervention, zero delegates approach us and two approach a purity speaker — demote machinery from headline to payload and run testimony-led strategy pending file 12 trigger T3.

### A2. "P3 abstain-tolerance is engineerable."
- **Shot:** US abstained on OUR OWN L.4 in Oct 2025. Tolerance is not default — it's transactional. In a school room the "US delegate" may be a random teenager who'll oppose anything multilateral for the fun of it. **FACT for drift; UNKNOWN for room behavior.**
- **Consequence:** We must hold a **bloc-size fallback**: text passes 55-65% without P3 smiles. Our vote math (02 §C forecast) says center+abolition+floaters ≈ 60-70% even if every P5-leaner sulks. Fallback viable **iff abolition merger closed.** Hence move-order dependency: merger closure is *higher* priority than P3 comfort theater. This **reverses the naive reading** of our war-brief — noted and locked.
- **Kill assumption fully:** if abolition merger fails AND P3-leaners hostile → we lose division-of-question fights → fall to Plan I (opposition-with-document) pre-written in war brief §18.

### A3. "China will attack via ALPS but verification judo neutralizes it."
- **Shot:** What if PRC delegate *accepts* universal verification loudly — "Lop Nur, come inspect" — because it's free in an MUN? Then our judo has no purchase and we're back to ALPS merits, where optics > IAEA PDFs. **HYPOTHESIS.**
- **Counter-in-kind:** If they accept universal verification, the *attack actually dies on its own* — a China conceding inspectability of legacy sites has conceded the norm we institutionalize in OP6/OP9. They win a news cycle; we win the text. Acceptable trade. **So the judo isn't the counter — the CONCESSION is. Be ready to celebrate their "yes" as our clause's first signatory.**
- **Kill condition:** If ALPS framing achieves three consecutive unpunished floor references without our successful redirect, chair sympathy likely tilting → fire pre-planned allied intervention (KAZ/MHL: "the poisoned states choose machinery over theater") rather than a fourth Japanese self-defense. *(Self-defense degrades by repetition; ally-defense doesn't.)*

### A4. "The September-2026 meeting is an unstealable hook."
- **Shot:** Kazakhstan/Kiribati delegates can mention it too — it's *their* resolution's meeting. If they brand it first in-room ("our meeting"), we're the appendage. **Plausible.**
- **Counter:** Speed + topic-specificity of our OP2-principles annex. Also flattery-custody: text says "building on the initiative of Kazakhstan and Kiribati" — named. **They keep their statue, inside our building.** If they still race the mention, our differentiation: we're the only ones who can credibly bridge to P3-tolerated *implementation* of that meeting's outcomes (US-abstention story recast: Japan's the one who can draft for abstainers).
- **Residual risk LOW after adjustment.** ✅

### A5. "Speed-of-filing wins by default."
- **Shot:** Filing first with mediocre text loses to filing second with excellent text + a rival's whisper campaign that ours is "rushed." **Plausible at quality-sensitive EBs.**
- **Counter:** File first AND FAMMS-aired. Our clause bank is pre-audited (war brief §15); the 90-minute discipline is only safe because homework pre-fills quality. Without the war brief, this assumption *would* be fatal. With it, acceptable.
- **Contingency:** if session-1 chair blocks early WPs (some circuits do), shift to **public annex circulation** (one-pager of architecture) to claim thought-custody without formal filing.

### A6. "ALPS batch-22 timing is survivable with discipline."
- **Shot:** If committee runs 12–17 August, and a delegate brings a phone with a live news alert mid-session ("BREAKING: Japan releases X tonnes"), emotional temperature spikes beyond scripted discipline. **UNKNOWN (committee date).**
- **Counter:** Pre-acknowledge in our own opening: *"Even this week, independent IAEA sampling accompanies every batch Japan discharges — transparency is not a favor we grant, it is infrastructure we built."* Inoculation > denial. One sentence, zero numbers-in-dispute, total ownership of the fact's interpretation.
- **Residual:** moderate; acceptable.
