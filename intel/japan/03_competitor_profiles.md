## D1 — 🇨🇳 THE PRC LINE (China + occasional DPRK/Iran echoes)
**Role:** Lead attacker. **Threat tier: 1.**

- **What they sell:** "Ongoing contamination accountability" — the reframe from *legacy assessment* to *live discharge*. Castle doctrine: all ecology roads lead to Japan's ALPS.
- **What they're actually good at:** Narrative consistency; patience; coalition-bribing of PIF/NAM with visibility offers; never being the one who looks angry.
- **What they're bad at:** Their own flank is enormous — Lop Nur (45+ tests, zero independent international access), CTBT signed-not-ratified, trilateral-talks reluctance (repeatedly rejected Japan's 2025 L.4 framework call), arsenal expansion (~600+ warheads, fastest growth). **[FACT — 09_evidence §C]** But *using* this on the floor risks us looking defensive; sovereign remedy is POI architecture, not speeches.
- **Growth signals:** 22nd ALPS batch live 12–17 Aug 2026 = their seasonal weapon; will open with "the ocean is not anyone's sewer" (their MFA's actual 2023 phrasing). **[FACT the framing exists; HYPOTHESIS the delegate will use it]**
- **Likely next moves:** (1) scope reframe in first moderated caucus; (2) PIF monetization offers; (3) "independent sampling" amendment; (4) tactical vote-merger with abolitionists.
- **Vulnerabilities:** (a) They co-signed our monitoring expansion (Sept 2024) — their attack re-validates our verification thesis; (b) any universal-verification amendment forces them to exempt Lop Nur publicly; (c) PIF states resent being *used* — China overplaying = jury backlash.
- **Attack surface in us:** ALPS window, umbrella, TPNW.
- **IF I WERE CHINA → how I beat Japan:** Reframe hour one; buy PIF custody; file rival chassis minute-91; poison-pill merger with liability verbs; POI TPNW twice daily; court USA privately with safety-clause bargain; amendment-hour "independent sampling" ambush; stay kind. (Full script: 00_verdict §⚔️.)
- **IF I WERE US → neutralization:** Speak earlier; close abolition merger-price first; PIF custody before auction; universal-application counter to every liability verb (their Lop Nur exemption = visible); 22-sec TPNW autopilot; pre-register USA with soft-OP3; substitute "IAEA-facilitated participation" for any sampling amendment; grace + the one number that kills ("twenty-one consecutive verified batches, including Chinese expert sampling").
- **Alert level:** 🔴 Red when (a) they say "Fukushima" pre-lunch day 1, (b) two+ PIF delegates in their huddle, (c) "independent" precedes "sampling" anywhere on paper.

**Role:** Moral incumbent & custody rival. **Threat tier: 1 (rivalry) / ally (votes).**

- **Bad at:** Passability. 170-4 still produced no fund, no mechanism, NWS-hostility baked in. Deep down they know September's meeting needs architecture and they don't have a drafter. **INFERENCE**
- **Likely moves:** (a) File moral WP early; (b) demand TPNW-fidelity preamble + their named OPs in any merger; (c) test us with "why no TPNW signature" in unmoderated (private) before floor (public).
- **Vulnerabilities:** (a) Their texts can't pass with P3 tolerance — structural, not fixable; (b) the September meeting (their own creation!) inherits *vague modalities* — our OP2-principles fill the hole they failed to fill themselves; (c) isotopic purity bores floaters.
- **IF I WERE THEM → beat Japan:** File at minute 60; make September-meeting custody the headline *of their text*; frame us as "the delegation that discharges and lectures"; force the TPNW question in a *moderated* caucus (where chairs score honesty); bid for PIF with purity, not plumbing.
- **IF I WERE US → neutralize:** The **timestamp + custody-share**: approach their frontrunner *before* their public launch; price the merger ourselves (preamble nod to TPNW's existence + they own the humanitarian/victim-assistance pillar *inside our chassis* + KAZ/KIR first-named co-sponsors). They can't refuse custody they've never been offered elsewhere. If they counter-demand TPNW-fidelity: walk, file ours, let them watch their September meeting get legislated by our text. They will come back in merger posture — they always do, because their norm without machinery is a eulogy.
- **SWING FACTOR:** Their delegate quality decides rivalry-vs-merger. Read in hour one: emotional framers = rivalry; procedural framers = merger.

If the delegate goes maximal-disruptor (test-resumption advocacy in-room), we do NOT defend them; we stay state-neutral and let the room's Western swing states carry water.
