## A. WHAT THE ROOM IS ACTUALLY BUYING (customer-jobs analysis)

Delegations don't buy "positions"; they buy **outcomes they can bring home**:

| Buyer | Job-to-be-done | Who sells it best |
|---|---|---|
| Legacy-affected states (KAZ, KIR, MHL, DZA, AUS-hist.) | Turn their UNGA-78/79-file into machinery + money | **We do** (only seller with fund + September linkage) |
| TPNW/abolition states | Moral vindication, treaty traction, on-record wins | Abolition core self-sells; *we* offer the only passing vehicle |
| NAM mass (IDN, BRA, ZAF, EGY, NGA…) | Capacity-building, no new obligations, visible leadership | Open auction — PRC buys with attention; we buy with authorship-share |
| Umbrella/Western states | Defensible text they can abstain-tolerate | Only our chassis is engineered for P3 tolerance |
| PIF states | Ocean protection + respect + resources | PRC will outbid on optics; we must win on *participation architecture* |
| PRC leadership project | A stage to prosecute "ongoing contamination" | They self-supply |
| Chairs/EB | A committee that shows: depth, procedure, diplomacy, a chairman's-pleasing resolution process | Whichever bloc runs the *cleanest drafting operation* — historically, the organizer-target we can own outright |

**SO WHAT:** We're not competing to be the most-liked country. We compete to be the **infrastructure through which everyone else's win flows**. Position: *the platform*, not *a rival product*.

## B. THE FULL COMPETITOR TAXONOMY (beyond the obvious)

### Tier 1 — DIRECTIONAL COMPETITORS (competing for the room's steering wheel)
- **PRC bloc-line** (PRC + potential DPRK/Iran sympathizers): product = "ongoing-contamination accountability" frame. Weapon = live ALPS window. Goal = convert agenda into Japan-on-trial.
- **Abolition core** (KAZ-KIR file owners + Austria/Mexico/Ireland veterans): product = moral-text + September-meeting custody. Not enemies — **competitors for the same franchise we need**.

### Tier 2 — CREDIBILITY COMPETITORS (competing for "sensible adult" space)
- **Swiss/Norwegian/Canadian-type bridge states**: no scar tissue, no contradictions, natural P3-tolerant drafters. If one of them files first, they become the platform *by being less attackable than us* — the genuinely underrated threat. **HYPOTHESIS (but the classic silent-killer at school MUNs)**
- **Australia**: legacy-affected (Maralinga) + umbrella + CTBTO champion = the closest *structural clone* of us. Either our best partner or our most dangerous substitute. Treat as acquisition target, not rival — absorbing them removes the substitutability.

### Tier 3 — FLOW COMPETITORS (competing for delegates' caucus-hours)
- **NAM anchor organizers** (India/Indonesia/Egypt/Brazil-type): whoever convenes the mass caucus controls quorum-level voting blocs.
- **The loud-floor generalist** (inevitable at school level): no bloc, big voice, moderates caucus topics toward drama. Environmental drama = résumé threat to our machinery agenda.

### Tier 4 — SUBSTITUTE "PRODUCTS" (not states — competing framings)
- "This committee should condemn, not assess" (abolition substitute)
- "This committee should regulate all nuclear activity" (scope-creep substitute — our worst)
- "This committee should just reaffirm NPT" (establishment substitute — kills our differentiation)
- "Crisis-adjacent drift" (if chairs inject scenarios — ZNPP-style updates pull debate toward drama and away from drafting)

### Tier 5 — POTENTIAL FUTURE COMPETITORS
- The **USA delegate if they wake up**: with one statement ("the US supports a verification-and-safety text"), inherits our entire coalition. Their Oct-2025 abstention posture says they'll more likely play hostile-to-machinery — *worse*: it drags us to babysit them.
- **France/UK**: silent now; if liability/compensation language gains steam they become active blockers with P3 gravity.
- **South Korea**: shares our RevCon-2026 frustration (verified) but also domestic ALPS anger — could tilt critic under domestic-roleplay accuracy. Watch.

### PARTNERS-THAT-COULD-TURN-COMPETITOR
- **Kazakhstan/Kiribati delegates**: today allies; if they smell award-size leadership, they file without us and relegate us to "funder mentioned in PP12". The countermove is *speed + custody-sharing on our terms* (file 08, move 2).
- **The delegate assigned "UNEP" or "IAEA-observer"** (interschool circuits love observer seats): whoever plays institutional-role becomes the chair's technical advisor — a shadow power. Worth recruiting as neutral-friendly witness for framework legitimacy.

## D. COMMODITIZATION ANALYSIS (where everyone sounds the same)

The phrases "critical threat," "global cooperation," "irreversible damage," "future generations" will be uttered 200+ times. Generic eco-condemnation is fully commoditized → zero award value. What is NON-commoditized:
1. Verified dated numbers with sources spoken aloud (rare at school level → chair-gold)
2. Procedural products (working papers that smell like UN texts)
3. Named-mechanism drafting (IMS stations, swatch: "radionuclide noble-gas detection")
4. September-2026 meeting linkage (our exclusive)
5. Bridging behavior between hostile camps (visible, rateable diplomacy)

**We hold 5/5 of the non-commoditized inventory pre-committee.** The operational question is only whether we spend it in order (file 08) or let others dilute it.
