# 19 — REBUTTAL PACK (every attack on Japan + the scripted kill)
**Format: the attack you'll hear → the floor version (≤25 seconds) → the deep ammunition. Say the floor version SLOWLY. Confidence is the argument.**

**A1. "Japan poisoned the Pacific — Fukushima ALPS water!"** (China's whole campaign)
- **Floor:** *"21 batches discharged. Every one independently monitored, every one far below the operational limit of 1,500 becquerels per litre — under IAEA arrangements that include Chinese experts since 2024. The delegate's concern is noted; the delegate's data is absent."*
- **Deep:** discharge began 24 Aug 2023; IAEA comprehensive review (Jul 2023) found the plan consistent with safety standards, negligible radiological impact; ~163,800 m³ released by batch 22; results published per batch. Tritium cannot be removed by ALPS — same as every operating plant on earth, incl. China's own fleet, which discharges many multiples of Fukushima's annual tritium budget. **[Known FACT base; exact Chinese-fleet comparison figure = quarantined unless verified — use qualitative "multiples" only]**
- **NEVER say:** "it's basically drinking water" (meme-able), or attack China's motives first — attack their data deficit.

**A2. "Japan sits on 45 tonnes of plutonium — latent bomb program!"**
- **Floor:** *"45.1 tonnes — of which 35.9 sit in the United Kingdom and France from 1990s reprocessing contracts. Every gram is declared annually under INFCIRC/549 and safeguarded by the IAEA. The three LARGEST civilian stockpiles belong to weapons states: UK 116, Russia 100, France 79. Japan is the only non-nuclear-weapon state that reprocesses — and the most inspected."*
- **Deep:** RECNA 2024 global inventory; AEC announcements; plutonium-use plan doctrine since 1997 ("no plutonium without a purpose"); MOX-burn + Rokkasho licensing caps. If pressed on Rokkasho commissioning delays: "licensing proceedings are transparent and ongoing; supervision intensity is the point."

**A3. "If Japan loves disarmament, sign the TPNW."**
- **Floor:** *"Japan lives between the world's only atomic bombings and the world's fastest nuclear buildups. We will not sign a treaty that our security environment makes irresponsible — and that no nuclear-armed state or its allies has joined. Instead we build the machinery everyone can stand on: monitoring, verification, remediation. That's why abolitionist states are co-sponsors of this very paper."*
- **Deep:** TPNW 75 parties (Jul 2026), zero nuclear-armed/umbrella states; Japan votes YES on humanitarian-impact items, abstains/no on TPNW-naming items; 117 Japanese lawmakers signed the pro-accession survey (Dec 2025) — proof of an honest domestic debate; our L.4 franchise = the bridge lane.

**A4. "Japan shelters under the US nuclear umbrella — hypocrisy."**
- **Floor:** *"Extended deterrence is a security fact, not an ecological position. This agenda measures contamination, not alliances. Japan's monitoring record, safeguards record and this paper stand on their own — co-sponsored by states under no umbrella at all."*
- **Deep:** umbrella ≠ arsenal; Japan's Three Non-Nuclear Principles (non-possession, non-production, non-introduction) since 1967; NPT NNWS in exemplary compliance.

**A5. "Japan is restarting reactors while talking safety — Kashiwazaki-Kariwa!"**
- **Floor:** *"Restart under the world's strictest post-Fukushima regulatory regime is not a proliferation story — it is exactly how a responsible state runs civil nuclear power: licenses, inspections, IAEA standards. This resolution distinguishes weapons legacies from regulated civil programs precisely so facts like these can't be conflated."* (PP8 lives here.)

**A6. "This paper duplicates Kazakhstan's September meeting — why twice?"**
- **Floor:** *"It feeds it. OP8 transmits this committee's machinery to the 1 September meeting as input — Kazakhstan is our co-sponsor and co-custodian of that pillar. Duplication is when you ignore the process; we're arming it."*

**A7. "Voluntary framework = toothless."**
- **Floor:** *"The CTBTO's monitoring system detected every DPRK test while the treaty itself remains unratified by key states. Voluntary machinery with verified data is how progress survives failed conferences. Teeth come AFTER instruments exist — the NPT itself began as scaffolding."*

**A8. "Why should the P5 accept a framework about THEIR weapons legacies?"**
- **Floor (to US/France/UK):** *"Nothing in this text names you, bills you, or binds you — it asks you to lend data and expertise you already publish domestically. A P5 state that can't support monitoring its own legacy sites should explain THAT to the committee."*

**A9. Chair-side challenge: "Isn't this really a disarmament speech in a lab coat?"**
- *"No, Chair — the operative section contains zero disarmament obligations. It builds assessment machinery: standards, networks, remediation practice. Whether committee members possess warheads or oppose them, they all need the same data layer. That is the paper's entire architecture."*

**A10. "TEPCO hid data for years — why trust Japanese data now?"**
- **Floor:** *"Exactly why the data regime changed. Post-2011, monitoring is IAEA-embedded, internationally participated, batch-published — including by the laboratories of skeptical neighbour states. Japan learned the hardest lesson on earth about opacity; that's why we wrote transparency into OP2 for everyone."*
