## 3. THE RESOLUTION WE PROPOSE

**Title:** "Global Framework for Nuclear-Ecological Monitoring, Response and Remediation"

### Preambular spine (PP1–PP11)
Charter purposes → ecology knows no border → KZ-KIR legacy resolutions (78/240, 79/60, 80/56) + the 1 Sept 2026 UN meeting → CTBT 30th anniversary (24 Sept 2026) → IAEA + 1986 notification conventions → UN Scientific Panel (2027 report) → NPT + TPNW "for their respective States parties" formula → **scope shield: weapons/testing/proliferation legacies, NOT civil operational discharges (IAEA's lane)** → Hiroshima/Nagasaki/Semipalatinsk/Moruroa testimony → vulnerable developing states → the world's only decade of post-accident remediation experience.

### Operative core (OP1–OP12)
- **OP1** — Japan (umbrella): *establishes* the Framework via existing institutions (IAEA, UNEP, CTBTO PrepCom), SG reports to GA-82.
- **OP2** — Switzerland: IAEA-verified data = international benchmark (the ALPS shield).
- **OP3** — India (+ Japan/Korea): voluntary international environmental monitoring network — air, water, soil — in cooperation with the IAEA, sharing radiation data.
- **OP4** — Philippines: transboundary early-warning + rapid-notification + mutual assistance for coastal/archipelagic states (built on the 1986 conventions).
- **OP5** — India (+ Egypt): capacity-building, South–South cooperation, training centres, radiation-monitoring infrastructure for developing states.
- **OP6** — Canada: consent-based radioactive-waste management best practices (deep geological disposal, safe storage/transport/monitoring).
- **OP7** — Germany (if in): decommissioning & post-shutdown environmental monitoring.
- **OP8** — Mexico + Kazakhstan: the 1 September 2026 meeting — high-level participation, outcomes to GA, voluntary contributions.
- **OP9** — Iran (caged): protection of safeguarded nuclear facilities from attack — IAEA GC 407/533 lineage, names no state.
- **OP10** — Brazil (if in): ABACC-style regional verification arrangements commended.
- **OP11** — UAE/Egypt flex: newcomer safeguards-by-design.
- **OP12** — follow-through: standing agenda review, remain seized.
