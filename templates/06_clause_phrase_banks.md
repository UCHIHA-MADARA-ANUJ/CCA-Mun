## 2. Operative Phrases (end with a SEMICOLON; final clause ends with a PERIOD; numbered)

Verbs ranked from weak to strong — pick the strongest you can defend, the weakest you need:

| Strength | Verbs |
|---|---|
| Weak / soft | Takes note of, Notes, Hopes, Considers, Suggests |
| Medium | Encourages, Invites, Recommends, Calls upon, Requests, Proposes, Emphasizes, Endorses, Supports, Affirms |
| Strong | Urges, Strongly urges, Calls for, Presses, Demands |
| Structural (create mechanisms) | Establishes, Authorizes, Decides, Directs, Designates, Transmits, Further resolves |
| Evaluative | Condemns, Strongly condemns, Deplores, Regrets, Reaffirms, Reiterates, Reminds, Confirms, Proclaims, Solemnly affirms, Declares |

Usage notes:
- **Condemns/Deplores** — only where the record is verified; a false condemnation destroys credibility.
- **Demands** — enforceability question: in a GA resolution it is still a recommendation; in the Security Council it can be binding. Use deliberately.
- **Establishes/Authorizes/Decides** — create the mechanism; pair with actor + funding + review or the clause is hollow.

## 3. Clause Anatomy

Every operative clause: `[VERB] + [actor] + [action] + [authority/mechanism] + [resources] + [timeline] + [monitoring]`.

Example:
"2. Requests the Secretary-General to establish, within 90 days, a voluntary trust fund for [purpose], administered by [body], with annual reporting to the committee;"

Sub-clauses:
```
2. Requests the Secretary-General to establish a coordination mechanism to:
   a) standardize data collection;
   b) publish quarterly reports;
   c) cooperate with [agency], including on capacity-building;
```

## 4. Amendment Language Starters

- **Add:** "Adds the following operative clause after clause X: '...'"
- **Delete:** "Deletes operative clause X, '...'."
- **Replace:** "Replaces operative clause X, '...', with '...'."
- **Split/Divide:** "Moves operative clauses X and Y into a separate draft resolution to be voted on separately." (division of the question)
- **Friendly vs unfriendly:** friendly = accepted by all sponsors (often adopted without debate); unfriendly = contested, voted on.

## 5. Safeguard Language Bank (protect red lines without losing support)

- "in accordance with national legislation and circumstances"
- "subject to the host state's consent"
- "with due regard for national sovereignty and territorial integrity"
- "including, where appropriate, technical assistance and capacity-building for developing countries"
- "with transparent reporting and independent oversight"
- "without prejudice to existing international obligations"
- "on a voluntary basis"
- "through existing mechanisms, where available"

## 6. Trap Words — Define or Bound Them

- **"Monitoring"** — who monitors, with what access, reporting to whom?
- **"Voluntary"** — does it gut the obligation your bloc needs?
- **"Binding"** — binding on whom, by what mechanism, with what consequence?
- **"Assistance"** — from whom, funded how, conditioned on what?
- **"Enforcement"** — authority? sanctions? who authorizes?
- **"Framework"** — a framework with no secretariat, fund, or timeline is a wish.

If a hostile amendment can reinterpret the word, the word is not yet safe. Define the mechanism in the same clause.
