# WORKING PAPER TEMPLATE — MUN COMMAND CENTER

A working paper is the bloc's pre-draft: organized ideas, not yet a formal resolution. It builds consensus, claims intellectual ownership, and becomes the draft resolution.

```text
WORKING PAPER 1.[number]
COMMITTEE: [Full Official Committee Name]
TOPIC: [Exact agenda wording]
SPONSORING DELEGATIONS: [name all who contributed]
DATE: [session date]

1. THESIS — the problem in one paragraph
   [What is broken, why it matters now, what the committee can realistically change.]

2. OBJECTIVES — 2–4 outcomes the bloc wants in the final resolution
   [O1. ... O2. ... O3. ...]

3. MECHANISM CLUSTERS (each cluster = future operative clauses)
   Cluster A — Immediate response / de-escalation
   - [action, actor, authority]
   Cluster B — Capacity building / prevention
   - [action, actor, funding route]
   Cluster C — Coordination / monitoring / accountability
   - [mechanism, reporting line, indicators]
   Cluster D — Safeguards
   - [sovereignty/consent/data/security protections]

4. FUNDING MAP
   - [Who funds what; donor base; fund manager; criteria]

5. UNRESOLVED BRACKETS (genuine disagreements — leave them visible)
   - [bracket 1: option A vs option B — who wants which]
   - [bracket 2: ...]

6. SPONSOR OWNERSHIP MAP
   - [Delegation] → leads Cluster [X]
   - [Delegation] → owns funding language
   - [Delegation] → recruiting [swing states]
```

## Rules for the Lobbying Phase

- Circulate the working paper early and invite additions — "add as you see fit and sign" builds authorship and goodwill simultaneously.
- Keep brackets few and real. A working paper with fifteen brackets is an argument, not a draft.
- Never show a rival bloc your paper's internal contradictions; merge language before it hits the floor.
- Convert to a draft resolution the moment a majority of sponsors is secured — do not wait for perfection.

## Pitch Card (for recruiting sponsors)

```text
WHAT IT DOES: [one line]
WHY IT PASSES: [which blocs support which clusters]
WHAT WE GIVE: [what each new sponsor can own]
WHAT WE PROTECT: [core clauses that do not move]
```
