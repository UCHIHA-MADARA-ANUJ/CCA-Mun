# POSITION PAPER TEMPLATE — MUN COMMAND CENTER

Format rules (conference requirements control — confirm before submitting):
- 1–1.5 pages per topic, single-spaced, 12 pt Times New Roman.
- Formal third person — never "I", "we", or "our".
- Header exactly as the conference requires.
- Works Cited: at least three sources NOT in the background guide, in the required citation style (commonly MLA).

```text
COMMITTEE: [Full Official Committee Name]
TOPIC: [Exact agenda wording]
COUNTRY: [Official Name of Country]
DELEGATE: [Name(s)]

1. TOPIC BACKGROUND (≈ 25%)
   [Opening sentence frames the debate from YOUR country's perspective — not a generic summary.]
   - Definition and scope of the issue
   - Recent international action: UN resolutions (correct symbols), treaties, agency reports
   - Why prior efforts fell short

2. COUNTRY POLICY (≈ 40%)
   - The national stance, stated explicitly
   - Evidence: official statements (head of state / foreign minister / permanent mission), voting records on past resolutions, domestic laws and policies, national actions taken
   - Why the issue matters to THIS country: security, trade, sovereignty, development, regional position, capacity
   - Red lines and negotiable areas

3. PROPOSED SOLUTIONS (≈ 35%)
   [Concrete mechanisms, not slogans. Each proposal answers: who acts, under what authority, with what funding, on what timeline, with what oversight.]
   - Proposal 1: [mechanism] — aligned with [country policy point]
   - Proposal 2: [mechanism]
   - Proposal 3: [mechanism]

WORKS CITED
[Source 1 — primary, with date and URL]
[Source 2 — primary]
[Source 3 — primary]
```

## Quality Bar

- Every factual claim verified and dated — no invented statistics, votes, or quotes.
- References at least three key documents not already cited in the background guide.
- Proposals pass the FAMMS test (Feasible, Authority, Measurable, Money, Safeguards).
- Position is consistent with the country's actual record (run the Foreign Policy Consistency Checker).
- No AI-generated or plagiarized content — conference rules commonly disqualify it.

## Paper → Podium Conversion (after submission)

Extract a one-page cheat sheet:

```text
STANCE: [one line]
FACTS: [3 verified facts with sources]
RED LINES: [3]
PROPOSALS: [3 with mechanisms]
OPENING SPEECH HOOK: [one line from the paper's framing]
```
