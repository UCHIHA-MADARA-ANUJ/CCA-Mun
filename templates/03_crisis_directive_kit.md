# CRISIS DIRECTIVE KIT — MUN COMMAND CENTER

Five instruments, five purposes. Specificity is everything: quantities, timetables, named assets, chain of command. Write for the staff to execute without inventing details.

## 1. Committee Directive (voted by the room)

```text
DIRECTIVE [number]
COMMITTEE: [Name]
SUBJECT: [one line]

The committee,
1. [action with actor and authority];
2. [action with quantities and timetable];
   a) [detail];
   b) [detail];
3. [funding/coordination mechanism];
4. [contingency/fallback].
```

Example: "1. Authorizes the deployment of a multinational observer mission of 250 personnel to [location] within 72 hours; 2. Requests the Secretary of [body] to coordinate logistics with [agency]; ..."

## 2. Personal Directive (your portfolio powers, not voted)

```text
PERSONAL DIRECTIVE — [Your Name, Your Portfolio]
POWER SOURCE: [background guide section / stated portfolio / defensible assumption]
1. [action with quantities and timetable];
2. [action];
3. [reporting: what the staff should confirm back].
```

Example (Minister of Finance): "POWER SOURCE: Portfolio — authority over national budget and financial assets. 1. Freeze all transfers to [entity] effective immediately; 2. Reallocate $50M to the disaster response fund within 24 hours; 3. Instruct the central bank to halt currency speculation against [currency]."

## 3. Crisis Note (private letter to a character — never voted, never public)

```text
TO: [character — ally, contact, subordinate]
FROM: [you]
[Opening line appropriate to the relationship.]

[WHO does WHAT, WHERE, WHEN, WHY, HOW — be explicit.]

[Closing: what you need back / how to confirm.]
```

Rules: address who/what/where/when/why/how; keep it short; never include a plan you do not want staff to know; tone matches the staff's style.

## 4. Joint Personal Directive / Joint Crisis Note (combine powers)

```text
JOINT NOTE — [Your Name, Portfolio] + [Partner Name, Portfolio]
POWERS COMBINED: [list both portfolios]
1. [action only possible together — e.g., Justice Minister investigates + Finance Minister freezes assets];
2. [division of labor: who does what];
3. [confirmation requested].
```

Choose partners deliberately: weak partners follow your lead; strong partners may betray you. Protect your covert assets — reveal only what the action requires.

## 5. Communiqué / Press Release (public narrative weapon)

```text
COMMUNIQUÉ — [Issuer]
To: [entity / the international community]
1. [statement of position or action];
2. [demand or invitation];
3. [consequence if ignored — framed diplomatically].
```

Use it to frame blame, set the narrative after a crisis, or signal to the other committee in joint crisis. Never put operational secrets in a public document.

## Portfolio Power Map (fill in before committee)

```text
MY PORTFOLIO: [role]
PUBLIC POWERS: [what I can order/do alone]
LIMITS: [what I cannot do — overstepping is rejected]
COVERT ASSETS: [connections, money, secret roles]
RESOURCE TRACKER: [uses remaining / staff feedback]
POWER UPGRADE PATH: [what I can unlock with demonstrated use]
```

## Update Response Sequence (run in minutes, not hours)

1. What changed? (confirmed vs rumored)
2. Which of my powers apply?
3. Draft one committee directive (circulate with "add and sign").
4. Send one personal move / crisis note advancing my arc.
5. Note the second-order consequence the staff may throw back.
