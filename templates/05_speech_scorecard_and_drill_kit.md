# SPEECH SCORECARD & DRILL KIT — MUN COMMAND CENTER

## 1. Timing Math (target ~10% under the limit, 120–140 wpm with pauses)

| Time limit | Target words |
|---|---|
| 20 s | 45–55 |
| 30 s | 70–80 |
| 45 s | 105–120 |
| 60 s | 140–155 |
| 90 s | 210–230 |
| 120 s | 280–310 |

## 2. Speech Skeletons

### 20 seconds (Frame → Evidence → Position → Action)
1. Frame: name the problem and the principle.
2. Evidence: one sharp verified fact or policy point.
3. Position: what the country supports or rejects.
4. Action: demand, offer, or propose a concrete move.

### 60 seconds
1. Hook and framing → 2. country interest → 3. evidence → 4. critique of insufficient approaches → 5. two-part solution → 6. coalition invitation.

### The 10-10 rule
- First 10 seconds scripted: hook + country + stance.
- Last 10 seconds scripted: the ask.
- Middle: modular blocks you can cut live if time shrinks.

## 4. Drills

1. **Cold-prep drill:** random topic → 3 min prep → 60 s speech. Do 5 per session.
2. **Timer drill:** every practice speech twice; second run must be under limit.
3. **Record-and-review:** count fillers; redo until zero.
4. **Pause drill:** force a 1-second pause before every emphasized word.
5. **POI-response drill:** partner fires questions; answer ≤ 15 seconds, always.
6. **Elevator pitches:** 15 / 30 / 60-second versions of your country position, from memory.
7. **Compression drill:** take a 60 s speech → 45 s → 30 s without losing the ask.

## 5. Live Q&A Response Formula (15 seconds)

1. Direct answer (one line).
2. One piece of verified evidence.
3. Redirect to your frame or the committee's next step.

Bridge line: "The delegation's position is X; the question's premise assumes Y, which is why we propose Z."

## 6. Pre-Speech Checklist

```text
FIRST LINE: [hook + country + stance]
KEY WORD + PAUSE: [one word to emphasize]
ONE FACT: [verified, dated, scoped]
THE ASK: [demand / offer / propose]
TIME BUDGET: [target words for the limit]
```

## 7. Nerves Protocol

- Box breathing: 4 in – 4 hold – 4 out – 4 hold, twice, before standing.
- First 3 seconds of eye contact on one friendly face.
- Recovery: if lost, pause → look at chair → continue from next module. Never apologize at length.
- Never invent a number live; use "the delegation will provide the precise figure in writing."
