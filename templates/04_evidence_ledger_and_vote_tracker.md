## 1. Evidence Ledger (from Core Protocol — maintain for all substantive research)

| ID | Claim | Label | Source | Date | Scope | Safe speaking line | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | [claim] | [VERIFIED FACT] | [link] | [date] | [who/where/when] | [line you can say aloud] | [limitation] |
| E2 | [claim] | [LIKELY INFERENCE] | [link] | [date] | [scope] | [line] | [risk] |
| E3 | [claim] | [CONTESTED CLAIM] | [link] | [date] | [scope] | [line] | [risk] |
| E4 | [claim] | [UNVERIFIED — DO NOT USE IN COMMITTEE] | — | — | — | — | [what must be checked] |

Grades: **A** = primary, current, direct, clearly scoped · **B** = high-quality secondary · **C** = context only · **D** = unusable.
No A/B evidence → the claim is not a central attack or speech fact.
