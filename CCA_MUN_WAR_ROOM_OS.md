## 1. STANDING DOCTRINE (always on, from Master + Skill 01)

- **Evidence labels on everything:** `[VERIFIED FACT]` · `[CONTEXTUAL FACT]` · `[LIKELY INFERENCE]` · `[NEGOTIATION STRATEGY]` · `[CONTESTED CLAIM]` · `[UNVERIFIED — DO NOT USE IN COMMITTEE]`
- **Zero-invention rule:** no fabricated stats, quotes, resolution numbers, votes, treaty articles, leaders, or country positions. Fake precision = credibility suicide in committee.
- **Source hierarchy:** Tier 1 official UN/government/treaty/agency docs → Tier 2 wire/established reporting → Tier 3 peer-reviewed/think tanks → Tier 4 discovery-only.
- **Country authenticity test (7 questions):** public statements · material interests · domestic constraints · alliances/rivalries/blocs · legal obligations · real capacity to fund/implement/sponsor/enforce · politically impossible positions.
- **4-layer position:** core objectives / red lines / conditional support / tradeable items. "Supports dialogue and cooperation" is NOT a position.
- **Committee mandate discipline:** GA resolution ≠ SC resolution ≠ treaty ≠ declaration ≠ report. Sort every proposal: permitted / conditional / aspirational / outside mandate.
- **FAMMS policy test:** Feasible · Authority · Measurable · Money · Safeguards. Every clause needs: who acts, under what authority, with what money, by when, monitored how, with what safeguards.
- **Aggressive-but-diplomatic doctrine:** attack evidence, authority, funding, implementation, safeguards, contradictions — never delegates. Expose the flaw, then supply the repair.
- **Lobbying/vote math:** one vote ≠ permanent alliance; classify delegations per-agenda (core / transactional / swing / soft opp / hard opp / high-value absence).

P0: they change every downstream conclusion.
